DHAKA, Sept 10, 2026 (BSS) - Information and Broadcasting Minister Andaleeve Rahman today said the government has no intention of suppressing free thought, freedom of the press or journalists through the proposed Cyber Security (Amendment) Act, 2026.

"The draft law is being revised with great care so that it does not contain any ambiguous (dual-meaning) words that could create opportunities for misuse against the media in the future," he said.

Earlier in the morning, in his opening remarks at the consultation meeting, the information minister said that before finalizing the Cyber Security Amendment Act, it is essential to engage directly with the media and seek their opinions in the interest of protecting the dissemination of information, independent journalism and freedom of expression.

"The level of cybercrime has increased alarmingly worldwide. In many countries, 44 percent of all crimes are now committed in cyberspace. In Bangladesh as well, there is no alternative to a timely law to prevent crimes such as online fraud, sextortion, blackmailing of women, and the disruption of social and religious harmony," he said.

Provisions for punishment of false complainants and the removal of non-bailable provisions are commendable, he stated.
