Home Minister Salahuddin Ahmed yesterday told parliament that the government has started the process to amend the Cyber Security Act to curb the spread of rumors, misinformation, defamatory content, and AI‑generated deceptive images, videos, and audio on social media.

He said the revised law will also include provisions to compel international technology platforms, including Meta (Facebook’s parent company), to remove harmful content within a specified timeframe.

“This very morning, we began drafting the amendment,” he said, stressing that the new law -- to be named Cyber Security Act, 2026 -- will redefine rumors, misinformation, and defamatory content, and introduce new penalties for their publication and dissemination.
