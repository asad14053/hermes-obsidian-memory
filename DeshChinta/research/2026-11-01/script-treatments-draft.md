Research base: [[DeshChinta/research/2026-11-01/editorial-dossier]].

These are **treatments, not completed scripts**. Proposed timestamps are structural allocations, not measured narration. Time the final copy after evidence refresh and legal/editorial review.

## Treatment 1 — চাকরির ভিসা থেকে যুদ্ধক্ষেত্র

### Thesis
একটি business visa বা পরিচিত এজেন্সির কাগজ একা নিরাপত্তার প্রমাণ নয়। জাতিসংঘের অনুসন্ধান দেখায়, বাংলাদেশিসহ বিভিন্ন দেশের মানুষকে বেসামরিক চাকরির প্রতিশ্রুতি দিয়ে রাশিয়ায় নিয়ে সামরিক চুক্তিতে বাধ্য করার প্রমাণ পাওয়া গেছে.[7][8]

### Opening hook
**“হাতে চাকরির ভিসা, সামনে নির্মাণকাজ—তারপরও একজন মানুষ কীভাবে যুদ্ধক্ষেত্রে পৌঁছে যায়?”**

On-screen qualifier: `জাতিসংঘ কমিশনের অনুসন্ধান; বাংলাদেশি মোট সংখ্যা প্রকাশিত নয়`.

### Narrative beats with recurring hooks

**Proposed 00:00–01:10 — কাগজটি দেখতে বৈধ**  
Open on a neutral specimen visa and job-offer graphic. The UN report says it received copies of business visas for Bangladeshi recruits naming a civilian Russian company.[7]  
Recurring hook: **“কাগজ সত্যি হলেও কাজের প্রতিশ্রুতি সত্যি কি?”**

**Proposed 01:10–02:20 — নিয়োগের ফানেল**  
Map the reported path: social-media or agent offer → visa and ticket → arrival → passport/phone control → Russian-language military contract → frontline. Keep every arrow labelled “UN Commission finding”, not a reenactment of one identified Bangladeshi.

**Proposed 02:20–03:30 — কী প্রমাণ দেখা হয়েছে**  
Show evidence categories: 245 testimonies, invitations, visas, travel records, contracts and official documents.[7]  
Recurring hook: **“এই সিদ্ধান্ত কি শুধু গল্প, না নথির ওপর দাঁড়ানো?”**

**Proposed 03:30–04:40 — কী জানা যায়নি**  
The report did not provide an exact Bangladesh victim, death or missing-person total.[9][10] Delete any thumbnail or narration that converts employment-clearance totals into trafficking totals.

**Proposed 04:40–05:50 — রাশিয়ার অস্বীকার**  
Report that Russia rejects the Commission’s jurisdiction and says the material is fabricated, while the Commission says Russia did not answer its written requests.[9]  
Recurring hook: **“অস্বীকার আছে—কিন্তু যাচাইয়ের জন্য কোন নথি প্রকাশ করা হচ্ছে?”**

**Proposed 05:50–07:10 — বাংলাদেশ কী করতে পারে**  
Use the Overseas Employment and Migrants Act’s pre-departure information right as the accountability baseline.[11] Ask for employer verification, contract-language access, visa-purpose matching, agent/sub-agent traceability, consular contact and public case-status reporting.

**Proposed 07:10–08:00 — দর্শকের চার-ধাপ পরীক্ষা**  
End with a non-exhaustive safety card: (1) employer independently verified, (2) contract translated before payment, (3) visa purpose matches work, (4) BMET/mission verification and family copy. Label it a public-safety checklist, not legal advice or a guarantee.

### Visual plan
- Clean document graphics; no staged battlefield footage presented as a real Bangladeshi case.
- A red “mismatch” line between civilian job and military contract.
- Evidence cards: `UN report`, `Bangladesh-specific business visas`, `exact total unknown`, `Russian denial`.
- Anonymous family silhouette only if needed; no identifiable victim media without consent.

### Counterarguments to include
- Some foreigners join armed forces knowingly and voluntarily; the Commission says it excluded those cases and focused on deception or coercion.[7]
- Russia rejects the Commission’s authority and conclusions; show that denial without letting it erase the Commission’s stated method.[9]
- Russia may contain legitimate civilian jobs; the video concerns documented recruitment abuse, not every Russia-bound worker.

### Evidence qualifiers
- Say `জাতিসংঘ কমিশন বলছে`, not `সব বাংলাদেশি কর্মীকে বাধ্য করা হয়েছে`.
- Do not use a Bangladesh casualty number unless an official, deduplicated list is obtained.
- Do not name a recruiting agency as guilty without original case records and legal review.

### Release gates
1. Refresh Bangladesh government action and Russian response.
2. Obtain any public investigation/case documents used in narration.
3. Legal review for named organisations or allegations.
4. Victim/family consent and image rights.
5. Verify every helpline or official URL on publication day.

## Treatment 2 — LDC ২০২৯: তিন বছরের জবাবদিহির ঘড়ি

### Thesis
UN date moved to 24 November 2029, but the same text makes the extension a reform-and-reporting window. The public-interest story is not “graduation cancelled”; it is “what must Bangladesh prove before the new deadline?”[1]

### Opening hook
**“বাংলাদেশ তিন বছর সময় পেয়েছে—কিন্তু ২০২৯-এ আমরা কী দেখিয়ে বলব, সময়টা কাজে লেগেছে?”**

On-screen qualifier: `উত্তরণ বাতিল নয়; প্রস্তুতির সময় ২৪ নভেম্বর ২০২৯ পর্যন্ত`.

### Narrative beats with recurring hooks

**Proposed 00:00–01:00 — তারিখ বদলেছে**  
Show 24 November 2026 crossed out and 24 November 2029 inserted, with A/81/L.8 on screen. Adoption reporting says the text passed by voice vote.[1][2]

**Proposed 01:00–02:10 — যোগ্যতা বনাম প্রস্তুতি**  
Bangladesh met graduation criteria in 2018 and 2021; the extension concerns readiness and structural vulnerability, not cancellation of those achievements.[3]  
Recurring hook: **“যোগ্য হওয়া আর ধাক্কা সামলাতে প্রস্তুত হওয়া কি এক?”**

**Proposed 02:10–03:30 — রেজল্যুশনের আসল হোমওয়ার্ক**  
Highlight three verbs: reform, implement, report. The UN text asks Bangladesh to advance domestic reforms, implement its smooth-transition strategy and provide annual updates.[1]

**Proposed 03:30–05:00 — পাঁচটি মাপযোগ্য বোর্ড**  
Propose a public dashboard: energy reliability, port/customs time, trade-deal status, export concentration, banking/revenue reform. Present these as editorial indicators, not metrics mandated verbatim by the resolution.

**Proposed 05:00–06:15 — সুবিধা কি অটোমেটিক?**  
Explain that preferences vary by market and partner rules; the extension does not automatically guarantee every tariff or financing benefit.[3]  
Recurring hook: **“UN-এর তারিখ বদলালেই কি প্রতিটি বাজারের নিয়ম বদলে যায়?”**

**Proposed 06:15–07:20 — সময় কেন নষ্ট হতে পারে**  
Present the strongest caution: extension without deadlines and public progress can become delay. Pair it with the counterargument that extra time can reduce adjustment shocks if reforms are actually delivered.

**Proposed 07:20–08:00 — দর্শকের স্কোরকার্ড**  
End on four questions: responsible agency, milestone, publication date, independent check. Invite public documents, not partisan slogans.

### Visual plan
- UN document zoom on the operative paragraph.[1]
- Countdown bar to 24 November 2029.
- “Eligibility ≠ readiness” split screen.
- Five-column reform scoreboard with `baseline / target / owner / deadline / progress`.
- Market-map labels `rule varies`; no universal “all benefits extended” graphic.

### Counterarguments to include
- Extra time can protect jobs and exports during a difficult transition; it is not automatically failure.
- Critics can reasonably worry that delay reduces reform urgency.
- Meeting UN criteria remains a development achievement even if readiness problems persist.

### Evidence qualifiers
- Use `preparatory period extended`, not `Bangladesh will remain LDC forever`.
- Treat investment, export and employment gains as projections.
- Verify the final adopted resolution symbol if issued; A/81/L.8 is the adopted draft text used here.

### Release gates
1. Recheck the UN LDC Portal and final resolution record.
2. Verify market-specific preference rules before naming a deadline.
3. Obtain the government’s latest transition roadmap and milestone list.
4. Trade/economics review for numeric impact claims.

## Treatment 3 — বোর্ড হয়েছে, মজুরি হয়নি

### Thesis
The 1 November decision starts a wage-setting process; it does not approve the workers’ Tk 30,000 demand or any other new minimum. The video should teach viewers how a wage board moves from representation and evidence to recommendation, declaration and factory-level payment.[4][5]

### Opening hook
**“৩০ হাজার টাকা মজুরি ঘোষণা হয়নি—তাহলে আজ সরকার আসলে কী ঘোষণা করেছে?”**

On-screen qualifier: `দাবি: Tk 30,000 | বর্তমান: Tk 12,500 | নতুন বোর্ড: গঠিত | নতুন মজুরি: নির্ধারিত নয়`.[5]

### Narrative beats with recurring hooks

**Proposed 00:00–01:00 — চারটি বাক্স**  
Demand, board, recommendation, final enforceable wage. Only the board box is newly checked based on current reporting.[4][5]

**Proposed 01:00–02:15 — বোর্ডে কারা**  
Reports name worker, employer, independent and chair roles, but the original ministry notifications were not directly retrieved.[4][5] Keep `reported composition / original notification pending` visible.

**Proposed 02:15–03:30 — আইন কোন তথ্য চায়**  
The Labour Act lists living costs and standards, production costs, productivity, prices, inflation, work risk, business capacity and socioeconomic conditions.[6]  
Recurring hook: **“কোন সংখ্যা নয়—কোন প্রমাণে সেই সংখ্যা?”**

**Proposed 03:30–04:50 — শ্রমিকের দাবি**  
Explain Tk 30,000 as the current demand, not an approved figure.[4][5] Ask what family budget, rent, food, transport, healthcare and savings assumptions support it.

**Proposed 04:50–06:00 — মালিকের সামর্থ্যের দাবি**  
Ask for audited order, price, energy, financing and productivity evidence rather than accepting “business is bad” as a complete answer. Do not invent an owner proposal that has not been published.

**Proposed 06:00–07:00 — রাস্তার সংঘর্ষ আলাদা ফাইল**  
Protests and clashes were reported, but responsibility for violence needs incident evidence and responses.[4][5] Do not let a process explainer identify attackers or legitimise force.

**Proposed 07:00–08:00 — কী ট্র্যাক করব**  
First meeting, submissions, draft recommendation, objection period, final gazette, implementation date, arrears and enforcement.  
Recurring payoff: **“বোর্ড গঠন শিরোনাম; মজুরি পৌঁছানো ফলাফল।”**

### Visual plan
- Process ladder with only verified stages checked.
- Six-seat board diagram using roles first; names only after notification verification.
- Cost-of-living and firm-capacity evidence scales.
- Factory-pay slip illustration labelled `specimen`, not a real worker document.

### Counterarguments to include
- Wage increases must protect living standards and employment sustainability.
- Export competitiveness is relevant, but it does not erase the law’s cost-of-living and inflation factors.[6]
- Road disruption does not determine the correct wage; evidence and representation should.

### Evidence qualifiers
- Never title the piece `গার্মেন্টস মজুরি ৩০ হাজার`.
- Say `workers are demanding`, not `the board decided`.
- Do not state a legal deadline until the notification and current amended law are checked.

### Release gates
1. Obtain the two ministry notifications and verify every member name.
2. Refresh first-meeting and protest status.
3. Seek worker and employer submissions or clearly say they are unavailable.
4. Legal review of the amended 2026 process before asserting deadlines.
5. Rights review for protest footage; no unrelated violence B-roll.

## Shared production safeguards

1. Every reel keeps its source/date card and uncertainty sentence.
2. Hooks may ask a question but must not smuggle in an unproved premise.
3. No casualty, wage-effect or trade-benefit number without source and method.
4. Use original graphics or cleared footage; a public URL is not reuse permission.
5. No outreach, legal advice, calendar booking or publication was performed.
6. The ten selections are the full requested slate; other ideas remain optional backlog.
