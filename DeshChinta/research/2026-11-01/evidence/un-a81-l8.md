United Nations A/81/L.8

General Assembly
20 October 2026

## Extension of the preparatory period preceding the graduation of Bangladesh and Nepal from the least developed country category

The General Assembly,

Taking note of Economic and Social Council decision 2026/345 of 21 July 2026, in which the Council took note of the Committee for Development Policy’s conclusions and suggested courses of action on the requests by the Government of Bangladesh and the Government of Nepal to consider extending their respective preparatory periods preceding graduation from the least developed country category by three years, namely until 24 November 2029, and recommended that the General Assembly take action on these matters before 24 November 2026,

2. Takes note of the view of the Committee for Development Policy that an extension of the preparatory period preceding the graduation of Bangladesh is appropriate provided that, during this period, Bangladesh advances significantly on domestic reforms aimed at addressing its persistent structural vulnerabilities;

3. Decides, on an exceptional basis, to extend by an additional period of three years, until 24 November 2029, the preparatory period for Bangladesh before graduation from the least developed country category occurs;

4. Invites Bangladesh to advance domestic reforms and continue to implement, during the extended preparatory period preceding graduation from the least developed country category, its national smooth transition strategy, with the support of the United Nations system and in cooperation with its bilateral, regional and multilateral development and trading partners, and to provide regular updates on implementation by including in its annual report to the Committee for Development Policy information on these efforts;
