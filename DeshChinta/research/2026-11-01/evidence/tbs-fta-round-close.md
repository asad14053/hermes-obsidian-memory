# Bangladesh, Malaysia conclude first FTA talks, next round planned for January

Bangladesh and Malaysia have concluded the first round of formal negotiations on a proposed free trade agreement (FTA), with the next round tentatively scheduled for January 2027.

The three-day talks, which began on 27 October, ended today (29 October) at Pan Pacific Sonargaon in Dhaka. Both sides reviewed draft chapters and explored ways to expand bilateral trade, boost investment and strengthen cooperation in technology and the digital economy.

Thirteen working groups examined draft chapters and structural issues related to the proposed agreement. Discussions covered trade in goods and services, rules of origin, customs facilitation, digital trade, sanitary and phytosanitary measures, technical barriers to trade, and issues affecting small and medium-sized enterprises.

The next round is expected to focus on resolving differences over individual chapters and reaching mutually acceptable positions.

While the first round marks progress in the negotiations, the terms of the proposed agreement have yet to be finalised.
