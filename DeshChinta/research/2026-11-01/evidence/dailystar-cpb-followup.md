# CPB demands justice after alleging attack on party office

 Published : 28 October 2026

•
 Updated : 28 October 2026, 02:03

The Communist Party of Bangladesh (CPB) and several left-leaning political organisations yesterday condemned what they described as an attack on the party’s central office, Mukti Bhaban, in the capital’s Purana Paltan, though police said no such incident had taken place.

In a statement, CPB President Kazi Sajjad Zahir Chandan and General Secretary Abdullah Kafi Ratan said attacks carried out on the basis of false accusations against people holding different political views undermine democracy, political coexistence and the right to engage in political activities freely.

They also demanded that those responsible for the attack be identified and brought to justice.

According to the statement, a group of people carrying sticks gathered in front of Mukti Bhaban on Monday night and attempted to force their way into the building by breaking its main gate.

The group identified itself as activists of the ruling party and alleged that members of a banned political organisation were staying inside the CPB office, the statement said.

When CPB leaders and activists tried to prevent them from entering, the group turned violent, leaving several people injured, it added.

The statement further alleged that the group continued to surround the building even after police arrived and took charge of its security. They also demanded that the alleged members of the banned organisation be handed over to them.

However, Shafiqul Islam, investigation inspector of Paltan Police Station, denied that any such attack had occurred.

Speaking to The Daily Star around 6:00pm yesterday, he said, “They [CPB] did not file any written complaint. There was no attack or vandalism.”

Dhaka South BNP Member Secretary Tanvir Ahmed Robin also questioned the allegations.

“It should first be verified whether the incident actually took place. If it did, we need to determine who was involved. But allegations against the BNP should not be made hastily. I do not even know whether such an incident occurred,” he said.

“They have not made any specific allegation either, which makes the matter confusing.”

In their statement, the CPB leaders noted that the party had participated in every democratic movement in Bangladesh and that many of its leaders and activists had sacrificed their lives in those struggles. They also said Mukti Bhaban had served as an important centre during the 2024 mass uprising.

Regardless of the political identity of those involved, such actions constitute an attack on democracy, democratic rights and freedom of political activity, they said.

The CPB leaders added that threats and attacks would not deter people’s aspirations for democracy. They said the public would assess the government’s commitment to democratic values based on whether those responsible were identified and brought to justice.

The Jatiya Samajtantrik Dal (JaSod) also condemned the alleged attack and the assault on CPB leaders and activists.

In a statement, JaSod described mob violence as a “national curse” and called for a united national resistance against such acts.

The party also alleged that police had wrongfully detained Dhaka Metropolitan North Jubo Union President Iran Mollah and General Secretary Sohel Fakir during the incident and demanded their immediate and unconditional release.

The left alliance, the Ganatantrik Jukta Front, staged a protest rally and procession over the incident. Senior alliance leaders joined a march in front of the National Press Club yesterday afternoon.

The alliance leaders called for an impartial investigation into the attack on the CPB office, the identification and trial of those involved, and adequate security for the offices of political parties and democratic organisations.

Meanwhile, Prime Minister’s Information Adviser Zahed Ur Rahman said the government would look into the alleged attack on the CPB office.

He made the remarks while responding to journalists’ questions during a briefing on government activities at the Secretariat yesterday.
