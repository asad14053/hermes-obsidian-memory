# Independent editorial desk review

## Overall assessment

The package is generally disciplined and publication-capable after targeted corrections. It separates confirmed, alleged, disputed and unknown material; avoids unsupported casualty totals; and correctly distinguishes a wage demand from an approved wage. The reel slate contains **exactly ten numbered, substantively distinct selections**: four trafficking, three LDC and three wage-board reels.

This review assesses only the supplied material. It does not independently verify sources and is not legal advice.

## Required fixes

### 1. Narrow the trafficking thesis to what the cited evidence establishes

Treatment 1 says people were taken to Russia and “সামরিক চুক্তিতে বাধ্য করার প্রমাণ পাওয়া গেছে.” That may accurately summarize the Commission’s broader findings, but the supplied Bangladesh-specific quotation establishes business visas naming a civilian company—not, by itself, that every Bangladeshi recruit was forced to sign a military contract.

Use:

> জাতিসংঘ কমিশন বলছে, বাংলাদেশিসহ ২৯ দেশের নাগরিকদের ক্ষেত্রে প্রতারণা বা জবরদস্তির মাধ্যমে রুশ সশস্ত্র বাহিনীতে নিয়োগের প্রমাণ পাওয়া গেছে; বাংলাদেশিদের বিষয়ে প্রতিবেদনে বেসামরিক প্রতিষ্ঠানের আমন্ত্রণ-উল্লেখসহ business visa-র কপির কথা আছে।

Apply the same distinction to the recruitment-funnel graphic. Each stage should be attributed to the Commission’s cross-country pattern; do not visually imply that every stage was documented for a Bangladeshi case.

### 2. Do not overstate what Russia denied

The dossier says Russia’s UN mission rejected jurisdiction and characterized the reporting as fabricated. Treatment 1 says Russia rejects the Commission’s “authority and conclusions.” Keep the denial as close as possible to the sourced formulation and identify who made it:

> রাশিয়ার জাতিসংঘ মিশন কমিশনের এখতিয়ার প্রত্যাখ্যান করেছে এবং প্রতিবেদনকে বানোয়াট তথ্যনির্ভর বলেছে—প্রথম আলোর প্রতিবেদন অনুযায়ী।

“Russia says” is broader than the evidence presented.

### 3. Correct the trafficking safety language

The hook “হাতে চাকরির ভিসা” is understandable, but a **business visa is not necessarily an employment authorization**. Calling it a “চাকরির ভিসা” risks reinforcing the misconception the piece is trying to correct.

Prefer:

> **“হাতে business visa, প্রতিশ্রুতি বেসামরিক কাজের—তারপর একজন মানুষ কীভাবে যুদ্ধক্ষেত্রে পৌঁছে যান?”**

Also replace “ভিসার উদ্দেশ্য কাজের সঙ্গে মেলে” with wording that does not imply a business visa lawfully authorizes employment:

> ভিসার ধরন ও অনুমোদিত উদ্দেশ্য প্রস্তাবিত কাজের সঙ্গে আইনসম্মতভাবে মেলে কি না।

The four-step card should not suggest that BMET or mission verification guarantees safety. Retain the explicit “non-exhaustive” and “not a guarantee” qualifications.

### 4. Fix unsupported or insufficiently cited specifics

Several statements require tighter pinpoint support:

- Treatment 1’s “passport/phone control” must be directly supported by the Commission report or removed.
- “The Commission says it excluded voluntary recruits” needs a pinpoint citation or quotation.
- The dossier’s “245 testimonies” and “recruitment from 48 countries” should identify the relevant report paragraph/page.
- LDC claims about annual CDP reporting, structural reforms and the relationship between eligibility and readiness need precise references to the operative text or clearly attributed secondary analysis.
- The wage-board process ladder—especially objection, declaration, gazette and enforceability—must be checked against the current amended law before presentation as the legally prescribed sequence.

A source list alone is not enough for high-risk or legally consequential claims. Add page, paragraph or statutory section references in the production script.

### 5. Reframe the LDC text’s “conditionality”

“Exceptional and conditional in policy terms” may imply that the 2029 date can be revoked automatically if reforms are insufficient. The supplied text supports reform and reporting expectations, but no stated revocation mechanism appears in the dossier.

Replace with:

> The extension is exceptional and accompanied by reform, implementation and reporting expectations.

Likewise, “what must Bangladesh prove” is rhetorically stronger than the evidence. Prefer:

> **“নতুন সময়সীমার আগে বাংলাদেশকে কোন অগ্রগতি দেখাতে হবে?”**

The proposed five-part dashboard is reasonable editorial analysis, but it must remain visibly separate from UN-mandated requirements.

### 6. Repair the wage-board evidence gate

Treatment 3 correctly says the ministry notifications were not retrieved, yet proposes publication soon after checking them. The release logic should be explicit:

- **No notification obtained:** publish only a narrowly attributed report-based explainer; do not present names, legal composition or deadlines as independently confirmed.
- **Notification obtained:** names, authority, date and terms can be stated after matching the document.
- **Current amended law not checked:** omit legal deadlines and any claim that a particular sequence is mandatory.

“Obtain the government’s latest transition roadmap” in Treatment 2 and “seek worker and employer submissions” in Treatment 3 are desirable, but they should not automatically block a factual explainer if the materials are unavailable and that absence is disclosed. Label them **enhanced-reporting gates**, not absolute release gates.

### 7. Make response-seeking proportional to the story

The dossier’s shared gate requiring institutional response before a “responsibility-focused” publication is sound but vague. Define the trigger:

- Required before naming an agency, company, official or identifiable person in connection with wrongdoing, negligence or disputed conduct.
- Not necessarily required for a document-led explainer that makes no new allegation and accurately includes already published responses.

Set a reasonable response deadline, preserve proof of outreach and state when no response was received.

## Bangla hooks and wording

Most hooks are accurate and natural. Required refinements:

- Replace mixed phrasing such as **“বৈধ-looking ভিসা”** with **“দেখতে বৈধ কাগজপত্র”** or **“আসল ভিসা”**, depending on the intended claim.
- Reel 2’s “ফাঁদটা কোথায় বদলে যায়?” is slightly imprecise; the situation changes, not the trap. Better:  
  **“নির্মাণকাজের প্রতিশ্রুতি কীভাবে সামরিক চুক্তির ফাঁদে বদলে যায়?”**
- Reel 10 presupposes valid representation: “বোর্ডে প্রতিনিধি আছেন” may imply representativeness rather than formal appointment. Better:  
  **“বোর্ডে শ্রমিকপক্ষের সদস্য থাকলেই কি শ্রমিকের প্রমাণ সিদ্ধান্তে পৌঁছায়?”**
- “আজ” in the wage hook will become stale quickly. Use the decision date or “এই ঘোষণায়” unless publishing the same day.
- Use one consistent style for `Tk 30,000`: preferably **৩০,০০০ টাকা** in Bangla-facing graphics.

## Repetition and differentiation

The three long treatments are adequately differentiated: personal safety and state accountability; macroeconomic implementation; and wage-setting procedure. The reels are also distinct, although trafficking reels 1–2 overlap around document mismatch. Preserve differentiation by making:

- Reel 1 a pre-departure verification checklist.
- Reel 2 an attributed explanation of the post-arrival coercion pattern.

LDC reels 5–7 and wage reels 8–10 have clear separate payoffs.

## Publication timing

The proposed windows are editorially plausible only if treated as freshness limits, not commitments. The strongest sequencing is:

1. Trafficking safety explainer after response and helpline refresh.
2. Wage-board explainer immediately after notification/status verification, because it is most perishable.
3. LDC accountability piece after the final resolution record and roadmap check; it is less time-sensitive.

Avoid publishing Treatment 3 merely “within the next few days” if the process has materially advanced. Replace relative windows with a final status check and a dated cutoff recorded in the script.
