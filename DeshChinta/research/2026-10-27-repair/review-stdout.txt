FACTUAL AND ATTRIBUTION REVIEW

Proposal 1

The core factual chain is represented accurately: source [31] reports the IGP’s statement, the Police Headquarters clarification, the Police Service Association position and the home minister’s rejection of the statement as government policy. Sources [50] and [51] support the constitutional wording; source [8] supports TIB’s criticism and demands.

The main vulnerability is second-hand evidence. Neither the complete speech nor the original police clarification is supplied. “ডেইলি স্টারের প্রতিবেদন অনুযায়ী” must therefore remain attached to those claims. TIB’s interpretation is advocacy evidence, not an adjudicated conclusion.

The title and opening hook are faithful because they pose a legal question rather than announce that permission was granted. The alternative title “কঠোর পুলিশিং আর বিচারবহির্ভূত হত্যা এক নয়” is accurate but less news-specific.

Legal risk is controlled reasonably well. Risk rises if “crossfire” is treated automatically as proof of a particular completed homicide, or if the IGP is personally labelled a killer or issuer of an operational order. None is established here.

Bangla is generally clear and publishable. A few legal paraphrases should track the constitutional text more closely.

Proposal 2

The proposal correctly distinguishes negotiations, a reported ToR signing, a future agreement and entry into force. It appropriately labels official claims about trade, investment and employment as expectations rather than measured outcomes.

The 30-versus-31 delegation issue is not strong enough for a standalone reel. The reports concern different dates and potentially different stages—arrival versus inaugural attendance—so the difference may not represent an error. Omit it unless its editorial purpose is broadened beyond this trivial count.

Reel 8’s warning about job offers is defensible but peripheral. No supplied source suggests the FTA grants individual employment or visa rights. It risks introducing a misconception primarily to refute it. Replace it with a cleaner distinction between trade commitments and labour-migration arrangements.

The proposed November 2–8 window is sensible because the scheduled round ends October 29. The requirement is a status refresh, not a demand that governments must already have published a round summary. Absence of a summary should be disclosed, not treated as evidence of secrecy or failure.

Bangla quality is strong and accessible. English terms such as “Rules of origin” and “Terms of Reference” should receive consistent Bangla explanations on first use.

Proposal 3

The proposal accurately presents an unresolved conflict rather than a settled attack finding. Sources [86] and [3] establish allegations, police presence, an early denial, a later CPB account, political condemnation and a government assurance. They do not independently establish assailant identity, injuries, custody facts or responsibility.

There is one meaningful attribution problem: Reel 1 says “সিপিবির কর্মীরা বলছেন হামলা,” while the early TBS account specifically attributes allegations to Chhatra Union and Jubo Union figures; the later account carries a CPB statement. These should not be merged.

The November 9–15 timing works only for a real follow-up: new records, official action, responses, or a clearly labelled missing-evidence audit. Replaying the October 26 allegation two weeks later without development would be stale and potentially unfair.

Defamation risk is highest here. Party and affiliate identities are allegations, not verified findings. Avoid faces, names or party insignia presented as convicted attackers. “স্থানীয় বিএনপি ও সহযোগী সংগঠনের লোকজন” must remain explicitly attributed. A right of reply is a genuine release requirement for any responsibility-focused version, not an optional enhancement.

TEN-REEL DISTINCTNESS

The documents provide 30 possible reel seeds, not 30 sufficiently distinct publication commitments.

Proposal 1 has approximately six distinct units. Reels 1, 2, 4 and 5 partially repeat the same statement/clarification/legal-limit contrast; Reels 8 and 9 both concern missing operational evidence. A tighter four-reel set would be:

1. Permission versus constitutional protection  
2. Statement, police clarification and ministerial position  
3. Lawful force versus extrajudicial punishment  
4. Missing speech, written instructions and accountability records

Proposal 2 has about seven distinct units. Reels 1, 2 and 10 overlap but can survive with sharper separation. Reel 9 should be dropped; Reel 8 should be reframed. Strongest four are:

1. Talks versus completed agreement  
2. Product coverage and tariff timing  
3. Rules of origin and practical usability  
4. Distribution of gains, costs and implementation

Proposal 3 has about six distinct units but only two currently have a sufficiently safe standalone purpose:

1. Precisely stated conflicting accounts  
2. Evidence needed to resolve the conflict

A defensible initial slate is therefore ten reels total across the three treatments—four crossfire, four FTA and two CPB—not ten releases from every treatment. Additional modules are optional enhancements after new evidence, not unmet requirements.

PROPOSALS VERSUS FULL SCRIPTS

The request represented in the supplied material is for script proposals/treatments, and that is what was produced. These are not full scripts: the narration seeds do not fill ten one-minute chapters, transitions and complete arguments are absent, and read time has not been measured. That is not a defect if proposals were requested. It becomes an unmet requirement only if the commission required recording-ready, timed scripts.

Genuine pre-publication requirements are source-status refreshes, accurate attribution, legal review for detailed use-of-force claims, right of reply for any CPB responsibility claim, and actual timing of completed narration. Full expansion of all 30 seeds, an executed FTA, and a future negotiation-round summary are not requirements that could already be satisfied on October 27.

INDEPENDENT-REVIEW STATUS

Desk review complete. Proposal 1 and Proposal 2 can proceed as limited explainers after their stated release checks. Proposal 3 needs further research or a narrowly framed unresolved-evidence treatment.

This review cannot authenticate the underlying websites, full IGP recording, original police documents, CPB incident records, medical evidence, signed ToR, later government decisions, copyright permissions, legal compliance, audience demand or developments outside the supplied material.
