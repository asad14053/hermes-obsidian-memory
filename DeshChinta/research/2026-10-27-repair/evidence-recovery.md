# Evidence-recovery addendum — 27 October 2026

## Resolution key

- **Recovered** means the text/media item itself was retrieved.
- **Reported text recovered** means a news outlet reproduced or directly quoted it; it is not relabelled as an original institutional publication.
- **Unavailable in this bounded check** means no publicly accessible original was recovered from the direct official/party routes checked; it is **not** a claim that the document does not exist.
- **Not-yet-due** is reserved for material whose stated event period had not closed by this cutoff.

## 1. IGP Ali Hossain Fakir remarks and Police Headquarters clarification

### Recovered

A contemporaneous TBS report preserves a direct attributed excerpt from the IGP: “Our responsibility is to apply the law and the Constitution wherever and whenever necessary. If a crossfire is necessary, and the government allows it, we will proceed accordingly.”[1]

A separate Prothom Alo report reproduces the Police Headquarters explanation that the term was used “in a literal sense” to illustrate strict enforcement, not to denote extrajudicial killing or punishment outside the judicial framework.[2]

### What this resolves—and does not

- The quoted crossfire passage is recovered as **reported text**, sufficient to document what the outlet attributes to the IGP.[1]
- The clarification’s substance is recovered as **reported text**; it is not an authenticated original Police Headquarters release.[2]
- **Still unavailable in this bounded check:** an unedited full speech, a complete event recording, the original dated Police Headquarters release/post, and any later correction, withdrawal, or operational instruction. The direct Bangladesh Police site timed out and the accessible Facebook route redirected to login; those failed-route captures are retained in `evidence/`.
- Do not call the reported excerpt a complete speech or call the reproduced clarification an original Police Headquarters document.

## 2. Mukti Bhaban / CPB confrontation

### Recovered

The best recovered incident record remains competing contemporaneous accounts. bdnews24 records the student-wing allegation that local BNP or allied actors attacked and tried to brand those inside as Awami League, while its report also quotes Paltan police saying there had been tension but “no assault or injury.”[3]

### Primary-evidence outcome

- **Unavailable in this bounded check:** an original CPB press statement dated 26–27 October, an original Paltan Police statement/log, a GD/FIR/case number, a medical record, authenticated original incident footage, a CCTV release, named accused persons’ direct responses, or an official arrest/custody record.
- The direct CPB web route did not yield a current incident post, and its public Facebook landing page did not expose a dated incident statement in the retrievable text. This is a retrieval gap, not evidence that CPB issued no statement.
- The reported allegation and the police denial remain distinct. Neither establishes attacker identity, injuries, party responsibility, complaint filing, or criminal culpability.[3]
- A search result exposed news-video links, but no source-authenticated original footage or medical document was recovered. Do not use those links as incident proof without provenance.

## 3. Bangladesh–Malaysia FTA, ToR, round status, and LDC deferment

### ToR and first round

bdnews24 reports that the two sides signed a Terms of Reference at the opening session “to set the framework and scope of the talks”; that is evidence of a reported negotiation framework, **not** a final FTA, tariff schedule, or entry-into-force instrument.[4]

The same account says the first round continues through 29 October.[4] At this 27 October cutoff, a round-end joint summary is therefore **not-yet-due**, rather than merely unavailable. A signed ToR file and authenticated ministry release were **not recovered in this bounded check**. The post-round official summary remains **not-yet-due** at this cutoff, rather than an overdue retrieval failure.

The Ministry of Commerce office-orders index (`/pages/office-orders`) was retrieved directly; it is not a comprehensive press-release archive. Its first displayed dated entry was an unrelated 25 October release order, not a Bangladesh–Malaysia FTA release.[6] That does not prove the ministry made no release elsewhere; it only records that this official index did not yield the requested original at the time checked.

### Current UN LDC-deferment status

The UN LDC Portal still lists Bangladesh as scheduled to graduate on 24 November 2026.[5] It records the Government’s request for an extension, the CDP’s conditional conclusion that an extension by the General Assembly would be appropriate, and ECOSOC’s recommendation that the General Assembly act before 24 November 2026.[5]

Accordingly, the retrievable official status is: **a General Assembly decision extending or otherwise changing the date was not recovered; the UN’s current portal continues to state the scheduled 24 November 2026 date.** Do not report a three-year deferment as decided, and do not turn the ToR into a completed FTA.[4][5]
