5. **Accountability:** Are takedown orders, rejected requests, wrongful removals, and reversals reported publicly?

Bangladesh’s Constitution protects speech and press freedom but permits reasonable restrictions for specified purposes.[5] The international standard adds a stricter test: restrictions should be clearly written, necessary for a legitimate purpose, proportionate, and subject to independent oversight.[4]

### The balanced conclusion

Cybercrime legislation is necessary. Deepfakes, fraud, impersonation, sextortion, and non-consensual imagery are not protected merely because they happen online. But technical offences such as hacking should not be casually combined with vague speech offences. That combination gives the agency built to fight cyberattacks power over journalism, criticism, and political discussion.
