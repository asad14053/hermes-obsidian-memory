# Writing Style

## Professional
- concise
- respectful
- natural
- not excessively formal
- Slack/text should sound like real messaging

## Scientific
- biological problem before implementation details
- exact quantitative results
- distinguish evidence from interpretation
- do not overclaim paper concordance
- plain explanation for mixed biology/neuroscience audience

## Language
- Bangla and English both acceptable.
- Bangla should be fluent and standard.
- Avoid awkward literal translation.
