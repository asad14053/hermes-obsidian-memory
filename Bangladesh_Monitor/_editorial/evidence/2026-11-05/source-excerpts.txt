The Office of the Chief Prosecutor of the International Crimes Tribunal (ICT) has written to the Ministry of Home Affairs, seeking implementation of the tribunal's order to confiscate the properties of former prime minister Sheikh Hasina and former home minister Asaduzzaman Khan Kamal, who were sentenced for crimes against humanity during the July Uprising.

A memorandum sent to the senior secretary of the Home Ministry regarding Sheikh Hasina says ICT-1, in ICT-BD Case No 02/2025, sentenced her to death for crimes against humanity on 17 November 2025. The tribunal also ordered the confiscation of all her properties and their distribution as compensation to the families of those killed and victims of the July Uprising.

Asked why the verdicts in the cases against Hasina and Kamal did not explicitly mention the confiscation of 50 percent of their assets, the chief prosecutor referred to the Rome Statute, which provides for confiscation orders covering up to 75 percent of assets.

Prime Minister Tarique Rahman has directed officials to advance plans for constructing a Y-shaped second Padma Bridge, a second Jamuna Bridge with priority given to northern connectivity, and the Padma Barrage project.

The meeting was informed that feasibility studies for the second Padma and Jamuna bridges were under way and expected to be completed by September 2027.

Final decisions on the locations, designs and estimated costs of the two bridges will be taken after the studies are completed.

Six more people died of dengue, taking this year's death toll to 439, and 2010 new patients were admitted to hospitals across the country in the 24 hours until 8am today (3 November).

According to the daily update from the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS), five died from Dhaka division and one from Barishal division. All of them died from Dengue Shock Syndrome.

SOURCE 7
Of the newly-hospitalized dengue patients, 943 were in Dhaka Division, 60 in Mymensingh Division, 245 in Chattogram Division, 235 in Khulna Division, 219 in Rajshahi Division, 65 in Rangpur Division, 219 in Barishal Division and 24 in Sylhet Division.

According to the report, a total of 140,909 dengue patients have, so far, been hospitalized across the country this year.

A recent death review by the Institute of Epidemiology, Disease Control and Research, led by its Director Prof Quazi Ahmed Zaki, found that more than one-third of the 119 patients reviewed died within a day of hospital admission.
