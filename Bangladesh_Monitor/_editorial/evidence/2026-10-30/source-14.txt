The United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) has approved Bangladesh's request for a three-year extension of its preparatory period for graduation from the least developed country (LDC) category, moving the deadline from 24 November 2026.
The resolution said the assembly decides, on an exceptional basis, to extend by an additional period of three years, until 24 November 2029, the preparatory period for Bangladesh before graduation from the least developed country category occurs.
It also urged Bangladesh to advance reforms, implement its smooth transition strategy with international support and report progress annually to the Committee for Development Policy.
