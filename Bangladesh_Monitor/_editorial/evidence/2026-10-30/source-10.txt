The family of an 18-year-old boy, who was shot dead in Dhaka’s Jatrabari on Tuesday evening, alleged that plainclothes members of Special Response Battalion opened fire during an anti-narcotics drive and caused the death.
Denying that SRB members had opened fire, Mohammad Asaduzzaman, commanding officer of SRB-10, told The Daily Star that drug peddlers attacked SRB members, snatched four detainees, and fired indiscriminately at the personnel.
“If any involvement of our personnel is found during the investigation, action will be taken against them,” he told The Daily Star.
