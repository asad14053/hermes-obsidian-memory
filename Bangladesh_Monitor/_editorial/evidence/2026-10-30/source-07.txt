The health directorate recorded the deaths of two children with measles-like symptoms in the 24 hours preceding 8am today (30 October), taking Bangladesh's combined toll of confirmed and suspected measles-related deaths to 1,251.
Of the total, 105 deaths were confirmed through laboratory tests, while 1,146 were classified as suspected measles-related deaths, according to data from the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS).
