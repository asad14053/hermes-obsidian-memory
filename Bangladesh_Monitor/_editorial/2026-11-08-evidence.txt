The country's largest gas distributor incurred system losses worth Tk2,719 crore in FY26 and Tk2,422 crore in FY25, bringing cumulative losses over the two-year period to Tk5,140 crore, according to the company's audited financial report.
Between FY20 and FY22, Titas Gas maintained system losses at 2%, well within the government's allowable threshold. However, losses began spiralling in FY23, climbing to 5.28% before rising further to 7.67% in FY24, 9.47% in FY25, and reaching 9.96% in FY26.
According to auditor ACNABIN, Titas Gas received 15,428 million cubic feet (mmcf) of gas in FY26 but sold only 13,892 mmcf.

A 2023 study by the Refugee and Migratory Movements Research Unit (RMMRU), based on Wage Earners’ Welfare Board (WEWB) records, found that labour-receiving countries repatriated 705 bodies of Bangladeshi women migrant workers between 2017 and 2022.
Of them, 110 deaths were recorded as suicide, including 68 in Saudi Arabia. Overall, 32 percent of the deaths were classified as unnatural, including accidents, murders and suicides.
WEWB documents obtained by The Daily Star show that 101 women’s bodies were repatriated in 2023 and 88 in 2025.
