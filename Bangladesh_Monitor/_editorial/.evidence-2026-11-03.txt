The revised bill, sent to the Posts and Telecommunications Division for approval last month, is 62 percent higher than the amount recommended in an earlier proposal for a licence period of 12 years and four months.
In its earlier proposal made on October 15, the regulator had recommended cutting spectrum prices by 10-30 percent, bringing the total renewal cost to around Tk 13,100 crore.
