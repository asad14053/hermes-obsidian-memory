# Bangladesh Ministry of Agriculture — September 2026

## Bottom line

**A new official September observation series was published:** the Department of Agricultural Marketing (DAM) posted daily Dhaka-market onion and potato price sheets for **1–30 September 2026**. The DAM landing page was last updated **5 October 2026, 13:23**; it lists 30 September and then 1–5 October observations.[1] The 30 September PDF itself is dated 30 September 2026; its PDF creation/modification metadata also reads 30 September 2026 (+06:00), but that is **not** proof of web-publication time.[2]

**No new September observation was found in the other principal official monthly series checked at the cutoff.** BBS's current inflation release remains for **August 2026**, and Bangladesh Bank's September *Monthly Economic Trends* issue carries Table VIII through **August**.[4][8] IMED's current FY2026-27 monthly ADP release covers **July–August**.[13] Bangladesh Bank's dedicated agricultural-finance and agricultural/non-farm rural-credit indexes remain at **June** and **July**, respectively.[9][10] This is a finding about the official pages/indexes checked on 6 October, not proof that no non-indexed document exists.

The Ministry of Agriculture's September-facing index material checked here is administrative rather than statistical: its notices index includes a 30 September meeting notice,[11] and its news index includes an 8 September condolence item.[12] Neither is a monthly data release.

## 1. DAM market observation: machine-readable comparison

**Coverage/definition.** DAM's sheet is headed: “ঢাকা মহানগরীর বাজার সমূদহর ৩০.০৯.২০২৬… পেঁয়াজ ও আলুর পাইকারী ও খুচরা বাজারের” — Dhaka metropolitan-market wholesale and retail onion/potato prices.[2] Unit: **Taka per kg**. These are quoted ranges, not national weighted averages; range endpoints should not be treated as an average transaction price.

| Commodity / market level | 31 Aug 2026 observation | 30 Sep 2026 observation | Verifiable endpoint comparison |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Domestic onion, wholesale | Tk **25–45/kg** | Tk **28–45/kg** | low end +Tk3 (+12.0%); high end unchanged |
| Domestic onion, retail | Tk **45–60/kg** | Tk **45–55/kg** | low end unchanged; high end −Tk5 (−8.33%) |
| Potato, wholesale | Tk **15–20/kg** | Tk **15–21/kg** | low end unchanged; high end +Tk1 (+5.0%) |
| Potato, retail | Tk **25–30/kg** | Tk **25–30/kg** | unchanged |

The August comparator is the prior calendar-month-end observation, **not a monthly average**. DAM's 30 September narrative says domestic-onion wholesale price was down **6.41%** versus 29 September, while retail onion and both potato levels were stable.[2] The 31 August sheet gives its own one-day comparison: domestic-onion wholesale +**1.45%** versus 30 August; potato wholesale/retail and onion retail stable.[3]

**Comparisons not made.** The examined official page did not provide a September-2025 counterpart, an FY2026-27 monthly average/YTD series, or a consistent 3–5-year history for these Dhaka range quotes. No year-on-year, FYTD, or multi-year price comparison is therefore presented.

## 2. BBS / Bangladesh Bank price context

BBS's latest visible national inflation release is explicitly **August 2026**: general **8.26%**, food **7.02%**, non-food **9.32%**.[4] Its paired release reports rural **8.31%** and urban **8.20%**.[5] BBS labels both pages as last updated **7 September 2026, 16:51**. The displayed “publication date” is **11-08-2026**, which predates the August observation month; treat that field as portal metadata rather than the release date pending clarification.[4][5]

Bangladesh Bank's *Monthly Economic Trends — September 2026* is a new September-titled publication in the official monthly index.[7][8] Its Table VIII (base **2021–22**) still ends the FY2026-27 series at August: general inflation **8.32% July / 8.26% August**, food **7.16% / 7.02%**, and non-food **9.28% / 9.32%**.[8] Thus the issue is useful corroboration of BBS's August figures, but it is **not September CPI data**. The PDF metadata modification time is 29 September 2026 UTC; Bangladesh Bank does not state a formal publication date on the index, so it is not reported as one.

- **Bangladesh Bank agriculture finance:** the dedicated *Monthly Report on Agricultural and Rural Financing* index's newest listed issue is **June 2026**.[9] The separate *Agricultural and Non Farm Rural Credit Position* index's newest listed issue is **July 2026**.[10] This is a measurable reporting lag relative to the September watch period, not evidence of missing disbursement or misconduct.

## 4. Claims and data-quality flags

1. **DAM monitoring claim.** The 30 September sheet says DAM's nationwide market-monitoring activity is ongoing. The sheet's numeric market evidence is nevertheless only the named Dhaka-market onion/potato range table; it does not establish national representativeness.[2]
2. **Catalog-label anomaly (DAM).** The page is typed “monthly report,” has original displayed publication date **29 July 2026**, and its table heading still says **“আগষ্ট-২০২৬” (August 2026)** while it lists observations through **5 October 2026**, including all 30 September dates.[1] This is a traceable metadata/heading inconsistency, not evidence that the dated daily PDFs are false.
3. **Release-date anomaly (BBS).** The August inflation pages show an 11 August “publication date” but a 7 September last-update time and August observations.[4][5] The sequence is internally inconsistent if the former is interpreted literally as the publication time; do not use it to time the release without a BBS clarification.
4. **Procurement / audit.** No agriculture-specific BPPA/e-GP award or CAG finding was machine-extracted and verified in this sweep. No procurement or audit irregularity is alleged.
