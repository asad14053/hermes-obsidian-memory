# GeneBridge — Goals

## Scientific

- Estimate unmeasured genes in Xenium using broader transcriptomic references.
- Preserve the spatial/cellular organization that gives Xenium biological value.
- Evaluate both expression fidelity and spatial fidelity.
- Select a method for cohort-scale disease analysis.
- Perform robust SCZ vs control differential expression.
- Compare/reproduce reference-paper disease signals where designs are comparable.

## Engineering

- Reproducible donor-wise pipelines.
- SLURM array execution across cohort.
- Clear separation of raw counts, QC, folds, imputed outputs, evaluation, and downstream DE.
- Preserve donor, diagnosis, sex, age, slide/run, and layer metadata.

## Communication

- Biological/spatial problem first; imputation is the enabling solution, not the protagonist.
