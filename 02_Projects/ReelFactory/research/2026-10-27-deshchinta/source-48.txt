Malaysia, Bangladesh Set To Start FTA Talks In Dhaka
27/10/2026 12:00 AM
By Shakir Husain
HYDERABAD, Oct 26 (Bernama) -- Malaysia and Bangladesh are set to start the first round of their free trade talks in Dhaka on Tuesday.
A 30-member Malaysian delegation is in the Bangladeshi capital for the three-day negotiations, Bangladesh media reported.
Bangladesh is keen to boost economic relations with its major trade partners as it prepares to graduate from the United Nations Least Developed Country (LDC) status next month.
“A free trade agreement (FTA) with Malaysia is of huge importance for Bangladesh's export competitiveness as after its graduation from the LDC status, preferential international market access will get affected,” Bangladesh-Malaysia Chamber of Commerce and Industry (BMCCI) president Mohammad Anwar Shahid told Bernama on Monday.
The two countries aim to conclude an FTA in 2027.
Bangladesh is Malaysia's second-largest trading partner in South Asia, with total trade in 2025 valued at RM12.18 billion.
Malaysia exported goods worth about RM10 billion to Bangladesh last year, with petroleum products constituting the principal export, while it imported Bangladeshi goods valued at RM2.1 billion, including textiles, apparel and footwear.
