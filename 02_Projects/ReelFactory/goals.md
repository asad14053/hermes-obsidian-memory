# Reel Factory — Goals

- High-volume generation with minimal manual work.
- Near-zero marginal inference cost.
- Separate research, scripting, asset planning, media retrieval, layout, audio, rendering, and QA.
- Preserve source references for factual/political content.
- Keep audio optional and reversible.
- Resume failed stages without restarting the entire pipeline.
