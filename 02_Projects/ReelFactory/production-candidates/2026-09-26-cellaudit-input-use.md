## TOPIC

A new CellAudit preprint reports an agent-selected cell-response predictor whose outputs stayed exactly unchanged when compound identity was replaced. Use that concrete case to explain why a predictive score does not, by itself, establish that a model uses the information it claims to use.[13]

Working Bangla framing: **যৌগের তথ্য পাল্টেও ফল বদলাল না: AI পরীক্ষার কোন ফাঁকটা ধরা পড়ল?**

This is a research-methods explainer, not a claim about a deployed diagnostic system or a treatment recommendation.

## CORE THESIS

A model's score, its sensitivity to an input, and the predictive value of that input are different questions. CellAudit tests them separately, connecting source-code inspection to controlled input replacements and measured prediction error.[13]

Editorial argument: when judging an AI discovery claim, ask not only “How well did it predict?” but also “Which information made the prediction better?” This is a narrower and more defensible claim than “AI does not understand biology.”

## COUNTERARGUMENT / ALTERNATIVE VIEW

**Strongest alternative:** perhaps the supplied compound feature is weak, redundant, poorly represented, or not useful for the selected target. Ignoring such an input is not automatically a bad prediction strategy. That is an interpretive possibility, not a measured explanation for every candidate.

**Response:** the question is whether the implementation and fitted behavior support the stated input-use claim. In the specific example, source inspection identifies an inactive compound route, and the matched control-only comparison makes the absent incremental value visible.[13] Avoid turning a useful falsification into an accusation.

**Give the field its strongest evidence:** the paper also reports revisions that recover positive compound contributions and predictive gains; its broader search-improvement results remain suggestive because the paired intervals span zero.[13] A positive input contribution still does not establish biological causality, and changing the input outside the relevant support can make a replacement test misleading.[13]
