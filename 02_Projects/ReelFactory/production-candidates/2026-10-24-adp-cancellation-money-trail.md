## TOPIC

**৯ প্রকল্প বাতিল: ১৫৬ কোটি খরচের কী হলো?**

DeshChinta explainer: trace the difference between a project's estimated cost, expenditure already incurred, usable assets or services, and obligations that remain after cancellation.

## CORE THESIS

প্রকল্প বাতিল মানেই পুরো প্রাক্কলিত ব্যয় নষ্ট হয়েছে, এমন নয়। আবার বন্ধ করলেই সেই পুরো টাকাটা সাশ্রয় হয়েছে, সেটাও ধরে নেওয়া যায় না। জবাবদিহির জন্য জানতে হবে: কত খরচ হয়েছে, কী কাজে লেগেছে, কী সম্পদ ব্যবহার করা যাবে, আর কী দেনা বাকি আছে।

This is the editorial interpretation, not an accounting finding. TBS reports estimated cost of Tk8,615.42 crore and expenditure of Tk156 crore across nine cancelled projects; neither figure establishes net loss or net savings.[1]

### Internal checks and unresolved contradictions

- The nine entries were counted programmatically. The reported proposal-category counts, 53 + 46 + 63 + 23, sum to 185; this checks internal arithmetic only.[1]
- Tk156 crore divided by Tk8,615.42 crore is about 1.81%, calculated in Python. This is an expenditure-to-estimated-cost ratio, not a waste rate, completion rate or forecast of savings.[1]
- TBS uses cancellation language but also describes committee recommendations. Without the dated orders, do not equate recommendation date, approval date, ADP deletion, contract termination and lender cancellation.[1]
- The third gas scheme is labelled “PGCBL” by TBS. The older World Bank record uses TGTDCL and PGCL for its residential-meter service territories, on pp.4–5. These are not identical identifiers, and project boundaries may differ. Preserve the mismatch; do not silently correct PGCBL or display an agency logo for that entry before the DPP is obtained.[1][9]
- World Bank's February record says the technical-assistance component was progressing and its development-objective rating was satisfactory, while October reporting describes current viability concerns. Different dates, components and evaluation measures prevent treating this as proof that either source is false.[1][9]
- No net savings calculation is supportable from the reported numbers. Benefits already delivered, recoverable value, termination obligations and future costs have not been reconciled.

## COUNTERARGUMENT / ALTERNATIVE VIEW

The strongest alternative is that cancellation is responsible loss prevention, not evidence of misconduct. Planning Commission officials frame the restructuring as a way to reduce administrative costs and duplication and redirect resources; TBS quotes Zahid Hussain calling the direction positive while warning about implementation and oversight risks, and Mustafa K Mujeri arguing the review may need to be broader.[1]

Give this view real screen time. Acknowledging past expenditure does not mean spending more is justified. Conversely, a project can have delivered something useful even if completing it is no longer worthwhile. The evidence needed is a comparison of future options and retained value, not a larger headline number. This is an analytical frame, not a project appraisal performed here.
