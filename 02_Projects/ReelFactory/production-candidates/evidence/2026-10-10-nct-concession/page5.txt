| Operation and Maintenance of CPA’s New Mooring Container Terminal (NCT) including Overflow Container Yard (OCY) by Private Terminal Operator under PPP. |
|  |
| Project Information | Details |
| **Project Name:** | Operation and Maintenance of CPA’s New Mooring Container Terminal (NCT) including Overflow Container Yard (OCY) by Private Terminal Operator under PPP. |
| **Sector:** | Port |
| **Department/Ministry:** | Ministry of Shipping |
| **Implementing Agency:** | Chittagong Port Authority (CPA) |
| **Location:** | Chattogram |
| **Approval:** | CCEA approved In-Principle Approval on 23-03-2023 |
| **Estimated Cost:** | USD 205 million (according to Feasibility Study report) |
| **Background:** | Chittagong Port Authority (CPA) plans to award a G2G concession to DP World, nominated by the Government of Dubai, for the upgradation, operation, and maintenance of the NCT.<br>CPA and PPPA have appointed IFC as transaction advisor to conduct due diligence, structure the transaction, draft the RFP and concession agreement, and facilitate negotiations with the selected International Terminal Operator. |
