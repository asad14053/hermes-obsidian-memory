# Data center-related ballot measures, 2026

In 2026, voters decided or will decide on at least **50 local ballot measures** related to data centers in 13 states: Arkansas, California, Florida, Illinois, Kansas, Maine, Michigan, Nevada, New Hampshire, New Jersey, North Dakota, Ohio, and Wisconsin.

## Analysis of November 3, 2026, election results

* Five full data center bans: one in California, one in Florida, and three in Ohio.
* Twelve partial bans: three in Kansas, one in Maine, and eight in Ohio. This includes bans with only narrow exceptions to bans on large data centers only.
* Three six-month moratoriums on data center development, all in Maine.
