# UN probe says Bangladeshis among nationals of 29 countries trafficked into Russian army
Bangladeshis were among nationals of 29 countries trafficked into the Russian armed forces through deception or coercion, with many lured by promises of well-paid civilian jobs before being sent to fight in Ukraine, a United Nations investigation has found.
Recruits were often forced to sign military contracts written in Russian, a language most could not understand; many were killed or went missing, leaving their families struggling to establish their fate, it added.
