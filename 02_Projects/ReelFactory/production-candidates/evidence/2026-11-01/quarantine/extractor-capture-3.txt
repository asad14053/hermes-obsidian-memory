# Russian authorities committed crimes against humanity through attacks on Ukraine’s energy infrastructure and trafficked foreign nationals to fight with their armed forces in Ukraine, says UN Commission of Inquiry
**NEW YORK / VIENNA, 26 October 2026** – Russian authorities have committed war crimes of intentionally directing attacks against civilian objects and the crimes against humanity of other inhumane acts with continuous attacks on Ukraine’s energy infrastructure , said the
Independent International Commission of Inquiry on Ukraine in a new [**report**](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/documents/hrbodies/hrcouncil/coiukraine/a-81-440-auv.pdf) submitted to the UN General Assembly.

...

The Commission also concluded that Russian authorities have recruited men trafficked from different countries into the Russian armed forces, in violation of international human rights law.
The Commission has investigated the impact on civilians of large-scale attacks by Russian armed forces on Ukraine’s energy infrastructure, particularly during the severe winter of 2025-2026, as well as their cumulative effect since autumn 2022.
The frequency, intensity and geographic range of systematic waves of attacks with hundreds of drones and missiles at a time have increased over the years and inflicted significant damage to Ukraine’s energy sector.

...

“As the cold season approaches, the attacks have intensified once again, raising serious concerns about the threats to civilians’ lives and health,” said **Erik Møse, Chair of the Commission.**

...

According to the Commission’s assessment, the Russian commanders ordering the attacks had the knowledge that the heating facilities they targeted in large civilian agglomerations were indispensable to the civilian population, particularly during the winter.

...

The evidence collected demonstrates that the attacks against Ukraine’s energy infrastructure were carried out with intent and pursuant to a state policy to deprive the civilian population of electricity and other essential energy-dependent services and cause suffering.
The Commission concluded that the Russian authorities have committed the crimes against humanity of other inhumane acts.
The Commission has relied on the accounts of 255 persons who endured winter under these conditions in Dnipro, Kyiv, Kharkiv, Kherson, Mykolaiv, Odesa, Poltava, Zaporizhzhia and Zhytomyr Oblasts, as well as Ukrainian authorities, medical doctors, and non-governmental

...

With regard to the issue of the recruitment of foreign nationals to serve in the Russian armed forces, the Commission found compelling evidence that Russian authorities have trafficked persons from 29 countries.

...

The Commission reiterates that international law prohibits attacks against civilians and civilian objects and calls for such attacks to stop.

...

_**Background:** The UN Independent International Commission of Inquiry on Ukraine is an independent body mandated by the UN Human Rights Council to, among other things, investigate all alleged violations and abuses of human rights and violations of international humanitarian
law, and related crimes in the context of the aggression against Ukraine by the Russian Federation. The Commission comprises Erik Møse (Chair), Pablo de Greiff and Vrinda Grover._
