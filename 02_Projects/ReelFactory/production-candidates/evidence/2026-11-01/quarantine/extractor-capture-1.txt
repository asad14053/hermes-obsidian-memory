# **Advance unedited version**
## _Summary_
The Independent International Commission of Inquiry on Ukraine submits the present report to the General Assembly at its eighty-first session.
The Commission has continued to investigate the impact on civilians of the large- scale attacks by Russian armed forces on Ukraine’s energy infrastructure, in particular during the 2025-2026 cold season, as well as the cumulative effect of these attacks since the outset. Over four years of systematic waves of attacks have caused intolerable living conditions for the civilian population.
Repeated strikes with hundreds of drones and missiles at a time have inflicted significant damage to Ukraine’s energy sector. Consequent outages have affected millions.
The scale of civilian suffering has been incremental over the years, due to the augmenting intensity of the attacks during the cold season, their widening geographic scope, and their repeated character. According to multiple interviews, persons living in the affected areas have developed various physical and mental health conditions as a consequence of prolonged exposure to cold in their homes.
Describing how her family coped with extensive outages during the winter, a woman from Dnipro city stated, _“Cold—it paralyzes you; there’s simply no way to get warm, and terrifying thoughts come into your_ mind”. As a new cold season sets in, the attacks have again intensified and there is serious concern for the life and health of civilians.
The Commission assesses that the Russian commanders ordering the attacks had the knowledge that the heating facilities they targeted in large civilian agglomerations were indispensable to the civilian population, in particular during the winter. Russian authorities have made official declarations concerning further attacks and referred to a plan for their commission.
The evidence collected demonstrates that the waves of attacks by Russian authorities against Ukraine’s energy infrastructure were carried out with intent and pursuant to a state policy to deprive the civilian population from essential energy supplies and cause suffering.
The Commission has concluded that the Russian authorities have committed war crimes of intentionally directing attacks against civilian objects and the crimes against humanity of other inhumane acts.

## **I. Concluding observations**
76. The evidence collected leads the Commission to conclude that the Russian authorities, in cooperation with recruiting agents, have trafficked persons from various geographic regions, in order to enlist them in the Russian armed forces in the context of the Russian Federation’s full-scale invasion of Ukraine, in violation of international human rights law.

...

77. The victims have been lured from abroad to the Russian Federation, by agents using deception, fraud and abusing their vulnerabilities, to entice them to accept false offers of employment. Further, agents operating around the world and cooperating with the Russian authorities have organized the transfer of recruits to the Russian Federation, through processing visas and arranging international travel, often for groups of recruits.
