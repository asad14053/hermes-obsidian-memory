# Factual claim audit

The October 12 Daily Star report describes a target to collect information on an estimated 4.45 crore families and add them to a database by December.[2]

The same report attributes pilot figures of 112,094 surveyed families and 71,831 selected beneficiaries receiving cards and allowances to the minister.[2]

The selected share of that reported pilot group is approximately 64.08%, calculated as 71,831 divided by 112,094 times 100; this is not a measured national eligibility or exclusion-error rate.[2]

The official SSPS overview describes targeted support using PMT scoring and door-to-door collection, not an unconditional payment promise to every surveyed household.[1]

TBS's July 14 account reports a 41 lakh new-card target for FY2026–27 and a 1.61 crore family coverage target by 2030, as historical government statements.[3]

BSS's October 12 account gives collection completion by November 30 and readiness by December as scheduled milestones, not a household application deadline.[4]

The BSS account describes an initial projected eligibility range of 16–21 million families out of approximately 45 million households; the relationship to earlier coverage targets remains unresolved.[4]

The source set supports a distinction between database coverage and benefit selection, but does not independently establish targeting accuracy or actual nationwide payment outcomes.[1][2]
