# Govt targets database of 4.45cr families under family card programme

 Nationwide data collection begins with over 66,000 enumerators; completion target set for December 

 Published : 12 October 2026 

 Updated : 12 October 2026, 22:15 

Photo: PID

The government has set a target to collect data from an estimated 4.45 crore families and add them to a database by December, as nationwide data collection under the Family Card programme began today.

A total of 66,815 data collectors and 6,681 supervisors have been deployed for the exercise, Social Welfare Minister Prof Dr AZM Zahid Hossain said at a press conference at the Information Secretariat on Monday.

State Minister for Social Welfare Farjana Sharmin was also present.

Dr Zahid noted that the prime minister inaugurated the pilot phase of the Family Card programme on March 10.

During the pilot phase, which ran through June 2026, data was collected from 112,094 families in 57 units across 55 upazilas and city corporation areas in 44 districts under all eight divisions, he said.

Based on Proxy Means Test scores, 71,831 eligible beneficiaries were selected and provided with family cards and allowances.

The minister said full-scale implementation of the programme began in July, with Tk 14,500 crore allocated for its execution in the 2026-27 fiscal year.

Dr Zahid said ensuring women's economic independence and advancing national development and social justice were among the government's key commitments.
