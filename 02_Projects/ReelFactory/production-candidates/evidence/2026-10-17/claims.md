# Source-bearing claim check

TBS reports that BERC set the private 12 kg LPG price at Tk1,837 on October 4.[20]
bdnews24's October 12 field report describes a Mirpur resident being quoted Tk2,400; this is a reported quotation, not an independently authenticated purchase receipt.[21]
The planned October 15–16 shutdown was withdrawn, according to bdnews24's October 14 report.[11]
The Daily Star's October 12 discussion report presents operator arguments about margins and supply, alongside CAB criticism of the regulator's handling of complaints.[22]
bdnews24 reports a hearing on October 29 and an October 27 deadline for written opinions and attendance registration.[31]
BERC's official homepage lists a public-hearing notice dated October 14; this run recovered the listing, not the attachment.[43]
The Family Card report describes a household-database target and separately describes pilot beneficiaries selected using PMT scores.[44]
The stipple abstract proposes distinguishing biological spatial variation from technical capture variation; it does not establish a causal cell-interaction claim.[15]
