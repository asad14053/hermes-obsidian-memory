# SIGNIFICANCE — 7

**Why should NIA care?** A defensible application would establish that a specific aging/AD-related exposure changes a consequential response in a defined neural/glial population, and that predicting that interaction improves biological understanding or experimental selection. A broadly useful virtual-cell platform with AD listed as a future application does not establish that premise.

The reviewed [[01_Research/research-overview|research overview]] centers on SCZ DLPFC spatial genomics. An age covariate in a schizophrenia analysis is not an aging mechanism. AD pathology, chronological age, inflammatory exposure and cell state must not be treated as interchangeable labels. An acute inflammatory experiment would require an explicit argument for the component of aging/AD biology it models and the components it cannot model.

**SCZ adds methodological preparation, not an automatic second disease aim.** GeneBridge can support donor-aware analysis, spatial measurement and a bounded conditional-prediction pilot. A second SCZ disease aim would dilute the NIA story unless there is a specified shared biological mechanism and an affordable, independent transport test. If SCZ remains the best-supported scientific question, retain that program rather than relabeling it to fit an institute.

# INNOVATION — 6

The potentially distinctive contribution is **measured, validated context-dependent response prediction with explicit limits of transport**, not assembling an identity encoder, attention, imputation and a conditional VAE. Those components are the proposal's implementation choices; their combination is not evidence of a new biological capability.

The October 2 radar already challenges state-expression accuracy, uncalibrated metrics and the assumption that an informative representation is actually used. Those implications are inherited from [[02_Projects/AIVC/reviews/2026-10-04-NIA-K99-study-section-review|the previous review]], not newly reproduced findings.

### New literature implications, bounded to retrieved evidence

- Yang and colleagues explicitly describe a feasible target-risk criterion using an **independent target evaluation sample**.[1] **Reviewer implication:** if this balancing approach becomes a comparator, specify exactly what target observations its tuning consumes. Distinguish a permitted target-calibration set from a protected final-test set, disclose access asymmetry between methods, and do not tune on final outcomes while claiming zero-shot transfer. This does not mean every form of covariate-only test adaptation is forbidden; it means the deployment contract must be stated.
- Wang and colleagues explicitly distinguish spatial domains from functional niches.[2] **Reviewer implication:** layer/domain agreement alone cannot validate AIVC's environmental representation. Compare prespecified proximity summaries and biologically motivated context features using held-donor measured outcomes, not merely cluster-label agreement. Do not optimize lineage weights against final evaluation labels and call the resulting agreement independent validation.

Neither paper supplies an aging/AD perturbation dataset, solves hidden confounding for this project, or warrants adding another core aim. No priority claim such as “first spatial virtual cell” is supportable from this review.

# CENTRAL HYPOTHESIS

The documented design contains a testable conditional-prediction premise: environment contributes information about activity beyond identity. It does not yet freeze a testable NIA-specific central hypothesis.

**Proposed hypothesis, not an adopted project fact:**

> In a prespecified human neural/glial population, a defined AD-associated inflammatory exposure modifies the response to a limited perturbation class; pre-intervention neighborhood programs predict that modification across held-out donors beyond identity and shared perturbation effects, and controlled exposure manipulation reproduces the predicted direction of a prespecified response program and functional endpoint.

Population, exposure, perturbation, timing, endpoint, meaningful effect size and assay access remain unselected. Inflammation is a narrowing proposal, not a validated aging model.

Falsification requires an informative design: a precisely estimated negligible interaction, no meaningful incremental context value with functioning sensitivity controls, or a successful manipulation contradicting a registered prediction. An insensitive assay, failed manipulation or underpowered estimate is inconclusive, not biological falsification.

# APPROACH — 8

## Estimand and identifiability

The PDF's identity-matched environment swap uses different observed cells. It is not paired counterfactual truth. Donor, layer, pathology, cell state and technical factors can explain differences despite embedding similarity.

```text
Conditional activity: m(c,e,x) = E[Y | C=c, E=e, X=x]
Simulated contrast: m(c,e',x) - m(c,e,x)
Intervention contrast: E[Y(e') - Y(e) | baseline C=c, X=x]
```

The last expression needs a well-defined intervention and justified identification assumptions or experimental assignment. An identity embedding measured after exposure can encode mediators or responses. Disjoint input/target genes do not by themselves solve this problem. Static snapshots do not establish dynamics.

**Correction to the October 5 strategy:** a full-rank design establishes algebraic estimability in the chosen model, not freedom from unmeasured confounding or adequate practical overlap. A context × perturbation coefficient may be estimable yet answer the wrong causal question. Require an exposure definition, confounder rationale, actual support and a stated intervention/observational interpretation in addition to rank.

## Data and leakage audit

- Split donors and linked modalities/sections before learning features, response programs, imputation, alignment or hyperparameters. Audit known reference/pretraining overlap and disclose unknown overlap.
- Separate donor generalization from gene, perturbation, context and platform generalization. GeneBridge's held-out-gene evaluation is not evidence of donor-held-out AIVC performance.
- Define deployment inputs. Observed test-tissue neighbors may be valid for conditional activity prediction; post-intervention neighbor outcomes cannot predict an unobserved future response.
- Exclude focal cells from neighborhoods; inspect segmentation spillover, density, boundary effects and spatially structured technical artifacts.
- Same donor does not imply the same section, spatial registration or paired cells. GeneBridge's dataset note explicitly preserves this distinction.
- Use measured targets for primary validation. Training and evaluating on the same imputer's pseudo-labels can reward reproducing that imputer.
- Tabulate donor × context × perturbation support, controls, dose, timing, batch and platform. Unpaired spatial and dissociated perturbation datasets do not automatically identify their interaction; a zero perturbation code is not a measured control for every context.
- Treat environment-only, perturbation-only and joint-change queries as separate claims requiring separate support. Unsupported combinations require an explicit restriction or abstention rule, not confident extrapolation.

Sources: [[02_Projects/AIVC/project-outline]], [[02_Projects/GeneBridge/datasets]], [[02_Projects/GeneBridge/methods]]. These are design risks, not bugs verified in an implementation.

## Proposed analysis and statistical safeguards

For the bounded measured-target SCZ pilot:

```text
M0: Y_u = alpha + B_C C_u + B_X X_u + epsilon_u
M1: Y_u = alpha + B_C C_u + B_E E_u + B_X X_u + epsilon_u
```

Use ridge regularization selected within training donors; C is permitted identity information, E is a prespecified context summary and X comprises deployment-available covariates. A small nonlinear extension is secondary. Freeze measured targets, normalization, radii, gene/program endpoints and model-selection rules before the final test.

For actual intervention data on a declared common scale:

```text
Delta_d,e,p = mean(Y_d,e,p) - mean(Y_d,e,p0)
Interaction_p = Delta_e1,p - Delta_e0,p
Prospective continuous functional endpoint:
F ~ context * perturbation + batch + (1 | donor)
```

Propagate treated/control uncertainty. The assay determines the actual response family, random effects, nesting, experimental unit and estimable covariates. Spatial interference may require assignment and analysis at culture/region level; cells are not independent biological replicates. Random intercepts cannot repair complete confounding.

Report final-state accuracy, shared perturbation response and context-specific deviations separately. Establish endpoint sensitivity with prespecified positive and negative controls. Outcome-informed calibration controls must not leak into deployable predictors or adaptive final-test selection.

**Audit of new weekly-plan thresholds:**

1. “Four of five folds” is a stability heuristic, not five independent replications; training sets overlap. Prespecify a biologically meaningful effect and uncertainty target. Distinguish donor variability conditional on fitted models from full pipeline uncertainty; if claiming the latter, resample/retrain the pipeline or justify the chosen approximation. Do not interpret a naive fold-level test as donor-level evidence.
2. Sign concordance restricted to measured nominally significant genes is a selected-subset diagnostic, not standalone validation. Report the full common-gene-universe coefficient relationship and uncertainty; recompute selection within resampling when making inference about the selected-subset procedure. The strategy's 80% threshold is a proposed planning rule, not a user-approved or empirically calibrated scientific standard.
3. A confidence interval excluding zero can support a small predictive difference without establishing biological usefulness. Freeze a meaningful-effect threshold after pilot variance/endpoint assessment, before protected testing. Distinguish negative evidence from insufficient precision.
4. Keep refitted no-context ablations separate from frozen-model context replacements. The former tests incremental information; the latter tests fitted reliance. Output movement and attention weights alone establish neither useful contribution nor causal mechanism. Restrict replacements to plausible support, and label out-of-support shuffles as stress tests.

These are reviewer-proposed corrections to [[09_Planning/weekly-strategies/2026-10-05-AIVC-GeneBridge-weekly-strategy]], not edits to adopted project decisions.

The attention notation, sparsity objective and conditional-VAE posterior/prior remain incompletely specified. If sparsity were implemented as L1 on nonnegative normalized attention, it would be constant; that implementation is not documented. Specification problems matter, but they are secondary to missing biological feasibility and executed evidence.

# TOP 3 EXPERIMENTS I SHOULD PRIORITIZE

## 1. Calibrated measured-outcome context-value test

After resolving feasibility, compare identity/additive baselines with pooled context and a minimal nonlinear extension on protected donor splits. Separate context-specific response from shared effects when intervention measurements exist. Compare measured outcomes, establish metric sensitivity and test both incremental context information and fitted reliance. Required output: data contract, predictions, sensitivity controls, biological-unit uncertainty and pass/fail/inconclusive decision.

If only GeneBridge is accessible, run the SCZ measured-activity pilot and label it conditional-prediction preparation—not AD, perturbation or causal evidence. Do not add target-calibrated methods without a separate declared calibration/test protocol.

## 2. GeneBridge disease-effect preservation and null calibration

Verify method versions, current output paths, donor/exclusion manifests and gene folds. Compare measured and held-out-gene imputed values on the same declared normalized scale and common gene universe:

```text
y_gdl ~ Dx_d + scale(Age_d) + Sex_d + layer_l + (1 | donor_d)
```

Audit design rank and confounding before adding run/slide terms. Freeze the formula before comparing methods. Evaluate coefficient direction, magnitude, calibration and uncertainty; use justified covariate-aware donor-level null resampling, not cell permutations. Include selected-gene sign concordance only as a transparent secondary diagnostic. Required output: traceable manifests, coefficient-concordance and null-calibration results, and a bounded downstream-use conclusion.

## 3. Limited prospective context × perturbation test

Secure one neural/glial system and an independently justified contrast. Randomize/control exposure and perturbation where feasible, verify manipulation, freeze predictions and measure both a response program and functional endpoint with biological replication. Required output: access/protocol commitment, pilot-based precision plan, measured interaction and uncertainty. Distinguish failed prediction from failed biological manipulation. No assay access means blocked feasibility, not permission to substitute in-silico validation.
