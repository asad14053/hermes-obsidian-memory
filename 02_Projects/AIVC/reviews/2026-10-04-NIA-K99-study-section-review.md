# SIGNIFICANCE — 7

**Why should NIA care?** A convincing application would explain how one defined aging/AD-relevant tissue environment changes a consequential cellular response, why current measurements or predictions cannot answer that question, and how the result would alter biological understanding or experiment selection. “A virtual cell that may eventually study AD” does not yet supply that chain.

Chronological age, AD pathology, inflammatory exposure, cell state, and dysfunction must be distinguished. An age adjustment in a SCZ analysis is not an aging mechanism. An acute exposure model would require an explicit explanation of which component of aging/AD biology it represents and which it does not.

**SCZ adds useful preparation, not an automatic second disease aim.** GeneBridge supports spatial measurement, donor-level inference, and a bounded engineering pilot. A parallel SCZ aim would broaden scope without a documented shared intervention-response mechanism. Keep SCZ as preparation or a later transport test only when the common mechanism is justified. If the strongest actual research question remains SCZ, preserve it rather than cosmetically relabeling it for NIA.

# INNOVATION — 6

**Potential contribution:** establish when measured tissue context adds reproducible response information across donors, at what biological resolution, and whether experimentally changing that context reproduces a predicted response contrast.

**Incremental framing:** combining pretrained identity embeddings, neighborhood attention, imputation, perturbation codes, and a generative decoder without showing a distinct capability. More modalities or a new architecture diagram do not establish innovation.

The October 2 radar and October 3 review already place recoverable response resolution, metric calibration, and useful-input auditing ahead of architecture novelty. This review carries forward their design implications; it does not claim a fresh primary-paper audit or independently reproduced literature results:

- Separate final-state prediction from shared perturbation effects and context-specific response recovery.
- Establish positive/negative-control separation before interpreting model rankings. An insensitive endpoint produces an inconclusive test, not proof that the biological premise is false.
- Distinguish information available in an embedding from useful reliance by the fitted model.
- Keep proximity-transfer and cross-species comparators optional; they are not reasons to add more core aims.

Provenance: [[02_Projects/AIVC/literature/2026-10-02-literature-radar]] and [[02_Projects/AIVC/reviews/2026-10-03-NIA-K99-study-section-review]]. No new external quantitative claims are made here.

**Most vulnerable claims:** counterfactual validation from matched different cells; causal mechanisms from attention; dynamics from static snapshots; universal transport from narrow datasets; and joint environment-by-perturbation prediction learned from unrelated unpaired datasets.

# CENTRAL HYPOTHESIS

The outline has a conditional-prediction premise—environment adds activity information beyond identity—but no frozen NIA-specific central hypothesis.

## One strong central hypothesis — proposed, not adopted

**In a prespecified human neural/glial population, a defined AD-associated inflammatory exposure modifies the response to a limited perturbation class; pre-intervention neighborhood programs predict that modification across held-out donors beyond identity and shared perturbation effects, and controlled exposure manipulation reproduces the predicted direction of a prespecified response program and functional endpoint.**

Inflammation is a suggested narrowing, not a selected or validated model. Population, exposure, perturbation, timing, response program, functional endpoint, and assay access remain to be established.

Falsifiers include a precisely estimated absent interaction, no added context information under sensitive evaluation, benefit reproduced by valid null-context controls, or a successful experimental manipulation contradicting the registered prediction. Failed manipulation or inadequate endpoint sensitivity is inconclusive, not biological falsification.

# APPROACH — 8

## Estimand and identification

The PDF's environment swap compares identity-matched but different observed cells. It does not supply a paired unobserved intervention outcome. Donor, layer, state, pathology, selection, and technical differences may remain.

```text
Conditional activity: m(c,e,x) = E[Y | C=c, E=e, X=x]
Simulated contrast: m(c,e',x) - m(c,e,x)
Intervention contrast: E[Y(e') - Y(e) | baseline C=c, X=x]
```

The third interpretation needs a defined intervention and defensible identification assumptions or an experimental design. “Identity” measured after exposure can contain response information; holding it fixed may condition on a mediator. Non-overlapping input/target genes do not alone solve this problem. Neighbor perturbations also require an exposure mapping and appropriate experimental unit because cells may influence one another.

## Data, confounding and leakage

- Split donors and linked sections/modalities before fitting feature selection, imputation, alignment, normalization parameters, response programs, or hyperparameters. Document pretrained-reference overlap when knowable and uncertainty when not.
- Define inputs available at deployment. Measured neighbors in observed test tissue may support conditional activity prediction; future response measurements cannot forecast their own unobserved response.
- Exclude focal-cell self-neighbors and inspect segmentation spillover, density, edges, and technical spatial dependence.
- Shared donor identifiers do not establish identical sections, registered coordinates, or paired cells.
- Evaluate against measured targets rather than the same imputer's pseudo-labels. Calibrate imputation confidence rather than assuming confidence weights are correct.
- Gene holdouts do not establish donor transport. Donor, perturbation, context, and dataset generalization are separate claims and need separate tests.
- Tabulate context × perturbation × donor support, controls, dose, time, and platform. Combining spatial and dissociated perturbation embeddings does not identify their interaction. A zero perturbation code does not provide a measured control for every environment.

These are design risks, not defects verified in an implementation: [[02_Projects/AIVC/project-outline]], [[02_Projects/AIVC/methods]], [[02_Projects/GeneBridge/datasets]].

## Proposed models and decision rules — not executed

For the measured-target SCZ pilot, compare ridge models on identical protected donor splits:

```text
M0: Y_u = alpha + B_C C_u + B_X X_u + epsilon_u
M1: Y_u = alpha + B_C C_u + B_E E_u + B_X X_u + epsilon_u
```

Here C is permitted identity information, E a frozen neighborhood summary, X deployment-available covariates, and Y measured held-out activity targets. Train/tune only within training donors. Compare a small nonlinear extension separately. A categorical unseen-donor effect cannot be learned for prediction on that donor.

For actual perturbation measurements, on a declared common scale:

```text
Delta_d,e,p = mean(Y_d,e,p) - mean(Y_d,e,p0)
Interaction_p = Delta_e1,p - Delta_e0,p
```

Use matched controls and propagate treated/control uncertainty. Report state accuracy, shared response, and context-specific deviation separately. Estimate prediction-side nuisance/shared effects using training data or cross-fitting. Test-derived responses can define evaluation truth but not prediction inputs or adaptive model selection.

For a continuous prospective functional endpoint, an initial model is:

```text
F ~ context * perturbation + batch + (1 | donor)
```

The assay determines actual nesting, randomization, response family, and estimable covariates. A random intercept cannot repair complete confounding or rank deficiency. Biological replication and precision must be justified from pilot variance, not cell counts; cells and cross-validation folds are not independent donors.

Before interpreting rankings, freeze the estimand, gene/program set, metrics, meaningful effect threshold, multiplicity family, and sensitivity controls. Keep outcome-informed calibration controls out of training and adaptive selection. Report pass, fail, or inconclusive according to precision and control behavior. Preserve prespecified fine- and coarse-resolution endpoints rather than selecting a favorable resolution after testing.

Refitted no-context ablations test the value of available information. Frozen-checkpoint support-matched replacements test fitted reliance. Require changes in relevant held-out loss/response recovery, not attention maps or output movement alone. Out-of-support shuffles are stress tests, not clean causal explanations.

The PDF's attention notation and the diagram's VAE/loss specification still require clarification. If sparsity were implemented as L1 on normalized nonnegative attention, it would not encourage sparse weights; that implementation has not been established. These specification issues are secondary to missing data and biological feasibility.

## 2. GeneBridge disease-effect preservation

Verify current method versions, donor/exclusion manifests and gene folds. Compare measured and held-out-gene imputed outcomes under the same declared normalized scale and repeated-donor model:


Audit design rank before adding any run/slide covariate; version the model before method comparison. Compare diagnosis coefficients, direction, calibration and uncertainty over a common gene universe. Use justified covariate-aware donor-level null resampling, not cell permutations. Required output: access/protocol commitment, variance-informed precision plan, measured interaction with uncertainty, and a distinction between prediction failure and biological falsification. If access is absent, record the blocker rather than claiming in-silico validation.
