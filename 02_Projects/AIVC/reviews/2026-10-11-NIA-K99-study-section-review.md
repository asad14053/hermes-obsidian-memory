# SIGNIFICANCE — 7

## Why should NIA care?

The proposal needs a specific explanation of how an aging/AD-associated context changes a consequential neural/glial response, and how predicting that change alters a biological experiment or mechanistic conclusion. “Virtual cells could eventually help Alzheimer's disease” is a motivation, not the premise of a focused application.

The documented current research maps SCZ molecular architecture in postmortem DLPFC, with spatial imputation and cohort-level analysis. This is relevant preparation, not established aging/AD response evidence. An age covariate, brain tissue or inflammatory annotation does not by itself establish an aging mechanism. Chronological age, AD pathology, experimentally induced exposure and disease-associated cell state must be distinguished. An acute exposure could model a bounded component of AD biology, but cannot simply be called aged human tissue. Source: [[01_Research/research-overview]].

## Does SCZ broaden or dilute the proposal?

Use SCZ/GeneBridge as methodological preparation and an engineering test bed. Do not make SCZ a co-equal disease aim without a specified shared mechanism and feasible transport experiment. Otherwise the applicant appears to be assembling diseases around a tool rather than answering an NIA question. If the strongest biological question remains SCZ, preserve that program and explicitly reconsider the NIA branch instead of relabeling the data.

# INNOVATION — 6

The potentially differentiating contribution is **experimentally checked context-dependent response prediction with explicit limits on transport**, not the combination of an identity encoder, neighborhood attention and a conditional VAE.

The [[02_Projects/AIVC/literature/2026-10-09-literature-radar|current radar]] and [[02_Projects/AIVC/reviews/2026-10-10-NIA-K99-study-section-review#New literature pressure|yesterday's source-checked novelty assessment]] already identify three relevant challenges:

- **VINTER:** neighborhood-conditioned prediction and editable molecular inputs are overlapping concepts. Annotation/input editing must not be conflated with measured perturbation-response validation.
- **Mechanisms Matter:** the recorded transportability work motivates response-contrast and diversity evaluation rather than relying on identity similarity or accurate average expression. More diverse predictions can still be wrong.
- **Latent causal diffusions:** mechanistic response modeling is competing work. A restricted identification result or held-out-combination benchmark is not unrestricted causal or unseen-tissue prediction.

These are carried-forward literature assessments, not newly discovered papers or a requirement to implement all three methods. Benchmark a comparator only when its endpoint, information access and supervision can be matched.

Reviewers would challenge “universal,” “causal counterfactual,” “dynamics from static snapshots,” “first identity–environment virtual cell,” and “therapeutic target prioritization.” Bound each claim to the actual intervention, population, endpoint and tested domain. Attention weights and edited inputs are model behavior, not identified regulatory mechanisms. A small model answering an important question would be more persuasive than a large architecture whose principal success is reconstruction.

> In one prespecified human neural/glial population, a defined AD-associated inflammatory exposure modifies the response to a limited gene-perturbation class; pre-intervention neighborhood programs predict this modification across held-out donors beyond cell identity and the shared perturbation effect, and controlled exposure manipulation reproduces the predicted direction of a prespecified response program and functional endpoint.

Inflammation is a candidate narrowing strategy, not an observed project result. The population, exposure, perturbation, dose/time, meaningful effect, endpoint and assay access remain to be selected. Until those are filled with evidence, this is a hypothesis template rather than a submission-ready premise.

Falsification routes include a precisely estimated negligible interaction, no meaningful context contribution under a demonstrably sensitive evaluation, or a successful controlled manipulation contradicting the registered prediction. Failed manipulation, insufficient support or imprecision is inconclusive. If a simple model succeeds and the complex model does not improve it, the biology may survive while the architectural justification fails.

# APPROACH — 8

## 1. Define what is being estimated

```text
Conditional activity: m(c,e,x) = E[Y | C=c, E=e, X=x]
Model-input contrast: m(c,e',x) - m(c,e,x)
Intervention contrast: E[Y(e') - Y(e) | baseline C=c, X=x]
```

These quantities are different. The PDF's identity-matched environment swap compares different observed cells and does not provide paired counterfactual truth. Matching can leave donor, layer, disease and technical confounding. An identity embedding measured after exposure may encode a mediator or outcome; disjoint identity/target genes alone do not establish disentanglement.

The diagram adds environment-only, perturbation-only and joint queries. Each requires its own data support. Unpaired spatial atlases and dissociated Perturb-seq do not automatically identify the joint environment-by-perturbation interaction. A zero perturbation embedding does not create a measured control in every environment. Static samples do not establish temporal dynamics. A full-rank regression design establishes algebraic estimability, not causal identification or practical overlap. Source: [[02_Projects/AIVC/project-outline]].

## 2. Data limitations, leakage and confounding

- Define deployment-available inputs before constructing folds. Observed neighborhoods may be valid inputs for conditional prediction in a new tissue. Post-treatment neighbor responses cannot be treated as pre-intervention information for predicting that same unobserved response.
- Hold out donors and all linked sections/modalities before fitting feature selection, normalization parameters, modules, imputation, alignment or hyperparameters. Audit reference/pretraining overlap and disclose unknown overlap.
- Separate gene, donor, perturbation, context and platform generalization. Held-out genes are not held-out donors; unseen combinations of known interventions are not unseen targets.
- Exclude focal cells from their neighborhoods. Check segmentation spillover, layer boundaries, density, region size and technical spatial autocorrelation.
- Resolve donor identifiers, tissue sections and registration separately. Matching donor labels do not establish aligned sections; the [[02_Projects/GeneBridge/datasets|GeneBridge dataset note]] also carries an unresolved donor-inventory warning.
- Evaluate primary endpoints on measured targets. Prediction of imputed pseudo-labels can amount to reproducing the teacher's errors.
- Tabulate donor × context × perturbation support, controls, batch, platform, dose and time. A random intercept cannot repair complete context–cohort or context–platform confounding.
- Use biological replication rather than cell counts for uncertainty. Synthetic samples are sensitivity analyses, not additional independent donors.

These are risks and requirements for a proposed design, not verified implementation bugs.

## 3. Statistical plan and model comparison

For a measured-target conditional-activity engineering pilot:

```text
M0: Y_u = alpha + B_C C_u + B_X X_u + epsilon_u
M1: Y_u = alpha + B_C C_u + B_E E_u + B_X X_u + epsilon_u
DeltaL_d = L_d(M0) - L_d(M1)
```

Here C is permitted identity information, E a prespecified numeric context summary and X permitted covariates. Use nested donor-grouped tuning, training-fold global and cell-type/layer means, and an unstratified pooled-context baseline. Compare richer context against the strongest simple model with the same information and tuning budget. Freeze target programs, transformation, neighborhood construction and metrics before final testing.

For measured intervention data on a declared common scale:

```text
Delta_d,e,p = mean(Y_d,e,p) - mean(Y_d,e,p0)
Interaction_p = E_d[Delta_d,e1,p - Delta_d,e0,p]
Functional endpoint: F ~ context * perturbation + batch + (1 | donor)
```

The interaction expression requires corresponding donor/context support or a justified alternative design. The actual assay determines response family, nesting and assignment unit. If cells affect their neighbors, treatment assignment and analysis may need culture/region units rather than pretending receiver cells are independent interventions. Propagate uncertainty from treated and control measurements.

Report final-state prediction, shared perturbation effects and context-specific response recovery separately. Pair primary loss with calibration, relevant response-program recovery and meaningful-effect/precision criteria based on pilot variance. A metric unable to separate prespecified positive and negative controls cannot adjudicate model equivalence or falsify the biological premise.

## 4. Validation safeguards carried forward

- Refitted no-context models test incremental information. Frozen-model context replacement tests reliance under a specified replacement distribution. Neither attention magnitude nor output movement alone proves useful contribution.
- Specify what a support-matched replacement destroys. Residual predictive gain is not automatically leakage if correlated information remains; the weekly strategy's universal disappearance criterion is too strong.
- Separate target calibration from protected testing. A transport-confidence score computed with final-test response outcomes is retrospective, not a deployable predictor. Give competing methods equal calibration access.
- Evaluate response diversity jointly with accuracy and a null control. Do not reward noisy, diverse responses over accurate shared-response predictions.
- Cross-validation folds with overlapping training sets are not independent biological replications. A fold-win count is a stability heuristic. Distinguish conditional test-donor uncertainty from uncertainty in the entire training/selection pipeline.
- Treat the weekly strategy's fold-win and selected-sign-concordance thresholds as proposed heuristics, not approved or calibrated standards. Selection on measured significance must be reflected in inference; report the complete eligible gene universe.
- Clarify attention dimensions, sparsity loss and the conditional-VAE posterior/prior before implementation. L1 would not sparsify normalized nonnegative attention, but no exact L1 implementation is documented; do not assert that bug exists.

# TOP 3 EXPERIMENTS I SHOULD PRIORITIZE

## 1. Measured-outcome context-value test

After the feasibility decision, compare identity/shared-response baselines against a small context-conditioned model under protected donor/context splits. Separate final-state, shared-response and interaction endpoints when intervention data exist. Include sensitivity controls, calibrated uncertainty and a declared calibration/test information boundary.

## 2. GeneBridge disease-effect preservation and null calibration

Verify current method versions, outputs, donor/exclusion inventory and gene folds. On identical declared normalized scales and a common eligible gene universe, compare measured with held-out-gene imputed diagnosis coefficients using the proposed primary model:

```text
y_gdl ~ Dx_d + scale(Age_d) + Sex_d + layer_l + (1 | donor_d)
```

Freeze the final estimable formula after a blinded rank/confounding audit; add technical terms only if justified and estimable. Assess effect direction/magnitude, calibration and donor-level uncertainty. Use covariate-aware donor-level null resampling, not cell permutations. Repeat outcome-dependent selection inside inferential resampling when making claims about selected genes.

**Deliverable:** file-traceable manifest, coefficient-concordance figure/table, null-calibration report and a bounded statement about downstream biological use. A reconstruction winner is not necessarily a disease-effect-preservation winner.

## 3. Controlled context × perturbation pilot

Secure a single neural/glial system and an independently motivated biological contrast. Randomize/control exposure and perturbation where feasible; verify manipulation; freeze model and baseline predictions; measure a response program and functional endpoint with independent biological replication. Match assignment and analysis to nesting and interference; use pilot variance for precision planning.

**Deliverable:** named access commitment, protocol, manipulation checks, interaction estimate with uncertainty and comparison to the registered prediction. Missing access is blocked feasibility, not permission to substitute in-silico outputs for experiments. Failed manipulation is not falsification of the response model.
