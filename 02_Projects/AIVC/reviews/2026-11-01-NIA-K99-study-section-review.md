# SIGNIFICANCE — 7

## Why should NIA care?

NIA should care only if the program answers a bounded aging question: **when and why does an aging- or AD-relevant tissue environment alter a defined cellular response, can that interaction be predicted across independent biological units, and can it be experimentally reproduced?** This could matter if the endpoint reflects vulnerability, resilience, or a modifiable neurodegenerative process.

The current program does not yet establish that chain. “AI virtual cell for brain disease” is a platform label, not an aging mechanism. The record does not select an aging/AD population, context or exposure, perturbation class, dose/time, response endpoint, functional consequence, or validation assay. Age metadata in postmortem tissue cannot substitute for an aging mechanism or intervention design.

## Aging, AD, and neurodegeneration fit

The NIA fit is aspirational. A credible premise requires:

```text
aging/AD exposure or state
  -> defined cellular vulnerability
  -> measurable response to a bounded perturbation
  -> molecular and functional consequence
  -> experimentally testable prediction
```

No link in this chain is currently frozen with verified access. Other laboratories' aging/AD studies in the literature radar are precedents or comparators, not the applicant's preliminary data.

## Does SCZ add useful breadth?

SCZ adds value as:

1. evidence of preparation in human cortical spatial genomics;
2. an engineering setting for donor-aware, measured-target prediction; and
3. a possible future transfer domain if a shared mechanism is explicitly tested.

SCZ makes the K99 unfocused if presented as a co-equal disease aim without a shared mechanism connecting SCZ and aging/AD. The canonical AIVC and GeneBridge records are SCZ-centered. Keep them accurately labeled. Do not convert SCZ prediction, imputation, or disease association into aging/AD preliminary evidence.

# INNOVATION — 6

## What is genuinely novel?

The defensible innovation would be **prospective recovery and experimental validation of a context-by-perturbation response in an aging-relevant neural system, with measurement-aware evaluation, transport boundaries, uncertainty, and abstention outside support**.

A second defensible contribution would be an evaluation framework that distinguishes:

- intrinsic state from local context;
- shared perturbation effects from context-specific interactions;
- final-state accuracy from response recovery;
- biological context from technical spatial structure;
- prediction from intervention claims; and
- useful uncertainty from uncertainty that merely tracks the predicted mean.

## What already exists?

The proposed architecture is assembled from established components: foundation-model embeddings, neighborhood aggregation, attention, multimodal alignment, perturbation embeddings, interaction features, and a conditional VAE. The literature index documents adjacent work in multiscale spatial representation, unified perturbational modeling, centered transfer, transport, uncertainty, donor-aware evaluation, variant-to-function screening, and interpretation controls. Consequently, “more modalities plus attention” or “graph plus transformer” is incremental.

## Claims reviewers will challenge

- **Universal representation:** no validated cross-system scope or failure boundary.
- **Counterfactual or causal prediction:** swapping observational model inputs does not identify an intervention effect.
- **Dynamics from snapshots:** cross-sectional cells are not longitudinal trajectories.
- **Attention reveals mechanisms:** attention is attribution, not regulatory proof.
- **Unseen-perturbation generalization:** performance may reflect perturbation similarity, pretraining overlap, response-head capacity, or test-information access.
- **Therapeutic prioritization:** requires protected prospective or orthogonal validation and a decision-relevant endpoint.

# CENTRAL HYPOTHESIS

No finalized NIA-specific central hypothesis is documented. The identity-environment concept is testable but too broad until one population, exposure, perturbation class, time/dose, endpoint, and validation route are specified.

## ONE STRONG CENTRAL HYPOTHESIS — proposed, not adopted

> **In one prespecified aging/AD-relevant human neural or glial population, a defined microenvironmental exposure modifies the response to a bounded perturbation class; baseline neighborhood programs predict that context-by-perturbation interaction across held-out biological units beyond intrinsic state, technical nuisance, and the shared perturbation effect, and controlled manipulation reproduces the predicted direction of a prespecified molecular program and functional endpoint.**

A precise null interaction or reproducibly opposite effect after successful manipulation would refute the premise. Failed manipulation, insensitive endpoints, inadequate overlap, or excessive uncertainty would be inconclusive. If an additive or ridge model succeeds while a graph/attention model adds nothing, the biological premise may survive while the architecture claim fails.

# APPROACH — 8

## Estimand and identification

The proposal must distinguish:

```text
Conditional prediction: E[Y | C=c, E=e, X=x]
Input contrast:         m(c,e1,x) - m(c,e0,x)
Intervention effect:    E[Y(e1)-Y(e0) | C=c, X=x]
```

The PDF's identity-matched environment swap pairs different cells. It does not provide paired counterfactual truth. Static tissue can support conditional prediction, but it does not by itself identify an environmental intervention. Unpaired spatial and Perturb-seq datasets do not identify a joint environment-by-perturbation response without additional design assumptions or measurements.

## Modeling assumptions

- Identity must remain stable on the intervention timescale; this can fail under differentiation, activation, aging-state change, or severe perturbation.
- Identity embeddings may encode diagnosis, target genes, post-exposure mediators, or batch. Disjoint input and output genes do not prove disentanglement.
- A zero perturbation vector is a parameterization, not evidence of a matched control.
- The conditional VAE prior, posterior, likelihood, and use of `L_identity` are underspecified.
- The PDF's attention aggregation and sparsity penalty are underspecified. L1 on normalized nonnegative softmax weights would be constant, but the exact penalty was not documented.
- Environment-only, perturbation-only, and joint queries require separate support.

## Data limitations and confounding

- No canonical AIVC dataset is selected.
- No matched donor/section inventory links the proposed Xenium, Visium, snRNA-seq, and Perturb-seq inputs.
- Shared donor labels do not prove section registration.
- GeneBridge's remembered 24-donor inventory remains incomplete; the listed names and Br8772 membership require reconciliation.
- Context, diagnosis/exposure, cohort, platform, dose, time, and batch may be aliased.
- Thousands of cells do not replace independent donors, animals, cultures, perturbation batches, or transductions.
- Density, layer, morphology, segmentation quality, and capture fields can be nuisance, mediator, or biology. Adjustment is a sensitivity analysis, not causal proof.

## Leakage

- Split independent biological units and linked modalities before normalization, imputation, module discovery, feature selection, alignment, nuisance estimation, and tuning.
- Audit foundation-model pretraining overlap where possible and declare unknown exposure.
- Use measured targets as primary truth; imputed-target agreement may reproduce the teacher.
- Exclude the focal cell from neighborhood summaries and test segmentation spillover.
- Define deployment-available inputs. Post-treatment neighborhood expression cannot predict an unobserved response if it contains the response.
- Avoid outcome-derived nuisance features and target-containing total counts in held-out-target evaluation.
- Keep outcome-informed metric calibration outside training, selection, and final testing.

## Baselines and ablations

Before a graph, transformer, or conditional VAE, compare under identical nested biological-unit splits:

```text
M0:  response ~ intrinsic state + design
MQ:  response ~ intrinsic state + design + target-independent nuisance
MQE: response ~ intrinsic state + design + target-independent nuisance + context
```

Also include no-change/control mean, shared perturbation mean, training-derived cell-type/layer means, nearest-context transfer, additive/ridge, centered transfer, pooled fixed-radius context, simple multiscale context, and cell-type-stratified context. Later perturbation experiments must factor perturbation representation, response head, and spatial context independently.

A refitted ablation tests incremental predictive information. Frozen-checkpoint replacement tests reliance under one replacement distribution. Neither establishes mechanism.

## Validation and statistics

A future controlled design should estimate an explicit interaction at the assignment unit, for example:

```text
Delta_d,e,p = mean(Y_d,e,p) - mean(Y_d,e,p0)
I_p = E_d[Delta_d,e1,p - Delta_d,e0,p]
Y_d,b,r = beta0 + betaE*E + betaP*P + betaEP*(E*P)
          + gamma_batch + b_d + epsilon_d,b,r
```

The assay must determine likelihood, assignment unit, repeated measures, dose/time, interference assumptions, and random effects. Report biological-unit effect sizes and intervals. Repeated cross-validation folds are not independent replication. State whether uncertainty conditions on frozen predictions or refits the full learning procedure. Prespecify multiplicity, practical effect sizes, positive controls, negative controls, and inconclusive outcomes.

Separate final-state accuracy, shared response, context-specific interaction recovery, abundance/composition change, uncertainty/error ranking, and functional consequence. A high expression correlation can coexist with failure to recover the interaction of interest.

## Biological validation gap

No aging/AD perturbation assay, organoid, primary-cell, ex vivo, or in vivo validation route is documented. An adequate pilot needs successful manipulation, independent biological replication, a prespecified molecular response, and a functional phenotype. Publications from other groups establish feasibility in their systems, not access or validity in this proposal.

# INDEPENDENCE

The durable independent question could be: **when do tissue environments alter cellular responses, and where do those response rules fail to transfer?** That could support a laboratory. As documented, however, AIVC may appear to be a technical extension of the mentor's spatial-integration program. The source PDF's “Thrust 3” label does not establish candidate ownership.

The application must document:

- candidate-owned aims, software, benchmarks, and biological questions;
- which datasets/resources remain with the mentor;
- written permission to continue the work after transition;
- portable assay partnerships and minimum R00 needs;
- intellectual separation from the mentor's integration methods; and
- an R00 launch experiment that does not require continued dependence on the postdoctoral laboratory.

## Can this become an independent program?

- **R01:** yes, after a replicated context-dependent mechanism and consequential endpoint.
- **NSF CAREER:** plausible if the general contribution is measurement-aware inference, transport, uncertainty, or benchmark design, paired with an education plan; this is not an eligibility conclusion.
- **Faculty laboratory:** plausible if a persistent scientific question links a bounded set of systems and iterative prediction-validation cycles. A catalog of diseases, modalities, and architectures is not a lab identity.
