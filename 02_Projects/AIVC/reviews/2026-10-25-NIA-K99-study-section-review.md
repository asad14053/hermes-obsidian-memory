# SIGNIFICANCE — 7

## Why should NIA care?

A convincing application would explain how a specific aging/AD-relevant environment alters a defined cellular response, why that response matters biologically, and what a validated model allows an investigator to do better. The present program provides the modeling concept but does not select this biological chain. “Virtual cells for brain disease” is a platform description, not the missing NIA premise.

The documented research foundation is SCZ in human postmortem DLPFC, with spatial integration, imputation, and donor-level analysis. It is not documented aging/AD perturbation evidence. Age adjustment does not turn an SCZ comparison into an aging mechanism. An inflammatory exposure needs an independently justified connection to the selected aging/AD question; the label alone is insufficient. Sources: [[01_Research/research-overview]], [[00_Profile/identity-and-career]], [[02_Projects/AIVC/datasets]].

## SCZ: useful breadth or distraction?

SCZ supports preparation and can serve as an engineering test bed or later bounded transfer contrast. Making SCZ a co-equal disease aim in an already underdeveloped NIA application would dilute the question unless the same mechanism and feasible experimental contrast connect the diseases. Preserve the canonical SCZ program. A separate NIA feasibility branch may legitimately end in NO-GO; grant fit is not a reason to rename existing evidence. Source: [[02_Projects/AIVC/goals]].

# INNOVATION — 6

**Potential contribution:** a measured, experimentally validated context-dependent response that generalizes within explicitly stated donor/context boundaries and improves a biological decision.

**Not yet a contribution:** combining a foundation-model identity encoder, neighborhood attention, perturbation embeddings, and a conditional VAE. The user supplied a simpler UR/VI PDF and a richer multimodal diagram; neither establishes implementation, and neither was declared to supersede the other. Source: [[02_Projects/AIVC/project-outline]].

The October 23 radar already raised the following challenges: Cole et al. separates perturbation representation from prediction-head complexity; GeoSinkhorn Flow supplies a conditional-distribution comparator; DynaVelo supplies a model-to-experiment rescue example in a different biological system; and the gate-importance study cautions against interpreting learned weights as mechanism.[1] These are attributed literature lessons, not applicant results or proof of neural/AD feasibility.

An eventual perturbation benchmark must vary perturbation embeddings and response heads separately, then test added spatial context with equal information access. Gene-perturbation representations are not cell-identity representations. A mean-response comparison cannot settle whether a distributional model recovers useful heterogeneity. This expanded benchmark belongs after response-data feasibility, not in the initial small GeneBridge context pilot.

Reviewers would challenge “universal,” “causal counterfactual,” “dynamics from snapshots,” “attention reveals regulation,” and “therapeutic discovery” unless each claim has its own evidence. More architectural components do not answer those challenges.

# APPROACH — 8

## Estimands and assumptions

```text
Conditional prediction: m(c,e,x) = E[Y | C=c, E=e, X=x]
Model-input contrast: m(c,e1,x) - m(c,e0,x)
Intervention contrast: E[Y(e1) - Y(e0) | baseline C=c, X=x]
```

These are not interchangeable. The PDF's identity-matched cells are different cells, not observed paired counterfactuals. Marker embeddings can contain outcome information, disease state, or post-exposure mediators; using disjoint target genes does not prove disentanglement. Holding identity constant after an environment change requires biological justification. Source: [[02_Projects/AIVC/project-outline]].

Static spatial snapshots and unrelated dissociated Perturb-seq do not automatically identify a joint environment-by-intervention response. The diagram's zero perturbation code is not evidence of an observed control. Distinguish environment-only, perturbation-only, and joint queries and show support for each claimed use.

## Data limits, confounding, and leakage

- Split linked donors and modalities before outcome-informed feature selection, imputation, alignment, module learning, nuisance estimation, or tuning. Audit reference/pretraining overlap, including unknown exposure.
- Declare deployment inputs. Measured neighborhoods can be legitimate for conditional prediction in a new tissue; post-treatment outcomes cannot be inputs for a not-yet-observed response.
- Make measured expression primary validation truth. Imputed targets risk evaluating agreement with the teacher. Held-out genes are not held-out donors.
- Exclude the focal cell from its neighborhood; audit segmentation spillover, boundaries, density, radius, and cell-type annotation. A donor match does not establish registered tissue sections.
- Verify the actual GeneBridge donor inventory. The dataset note explicitly flags its remembered list as incomplete; do not reconstruct cohort membership from that list. Source: [[02_Projects/GeneBridge/datasets]].
- Tabulate context × perturbation × biological unit and controls, batch/platform, dose, and time. A full-rank design does not establish causal identification or practical overlap; random effects cannot repair complete context–cohort confounding.
- Compare nuisance-aware simple models. Reproducible technical spatial structure can survive donor holdout. Target-containing library totals may violate a held-out-target contract. Density/morphology may also be real biology: nuisance adjustment is a sensitivity analysis, not proof of causality.

## First conditional-activity pilot

Use regularized, measured-target models with identical nested donor-grouped splits:

```text
M0:  Y_u = alpha + B_C*C_u + B_X*X_u + epsilon_u
MQ:  Y_u = alpha + B_C*C_u + B_X*X_u + B_Q*Q_u + epsilon_u
MQE: Y_u = alpha + B_C*C_u + B_X*X_u + B_Q*Q_u + B_E*E_u + epsilon_u
DeltaL_d = L_d(strongest eligible baseline) - L_d(context model)
```

C is permitted identity input, X design information, Q justified target-independent nuisance, and E frozen context features. Include training-derived cell-type/layer means, pooled fixed-radius context, simple multiscale context, and cell-type-stratified context. Select the strongest comparator without final-test outcomes. Match the loss to the measured target.

Refitted context removal measures added predictive information; frozen-checkpoint replacement measures reliance under the chosen replacement distribution. Neither identifies a biological mechanism. Support-matched replacement may retain correlated information, so residual gain is not automatically leakage. Attention values and output movement alone are insufficient validation.

## Response validation, statistics, and generalization

For an eligible controlled design on a declared endpoint scale:

```text
Delta_d,e,p = mean(Y_d,e,p) - mean(Y_d,e,p0)
I_p = E_d[Delta_d,e1,p - Delta_d,e0,p]
Y_d,b,r = beta0 + betaE*E + betaP*P + betaEP*(E*P)
          + gamma_batch + b_d + epsilon_d,b,r
```

The final line is an illustrative continuous-outcome model, not a frozen formula for an unknown assay. Specify assignment unit, repeated measures, likelihood, random effects, dose/time, and interference from the actual design. Cells and technical wells do not substitute for independent biological units. Where supported, model donor-response heterogeneity.

Separate final-state prediction, shared response, context-specific interaction recovery, and abundance change. Increased response diversity can be noise, while mean accuracy can miss relevant heterogeneity. Use prespecified sensitivity controls and null calibration, with outcome-informed calibration isolated from protected model selection/testing. Match multiplicity control to the declared endpoint and gene family.

Report biological-unit effect sizes and uncertainty. Repeated folds share training sets; fold wins are not independent biological replications. State whether bootstrap intervals condition on fixed predictions or refit the complete learning pipeline. The former does not include all model-selection/training uncertainty. Set practical effect thresholds from assay/control variability rather than adopting an arbitrary percentage or merely requiring a nonzero interval.

The PDF's attention aggregation shape and sparsity penalty remain underspecified. L1 on nonnegative normalized softmax weights would be constant, but L1 was not specified; this is a conditional warning, not a verified implementation bug. The diagram also leaves conditional-VAE prior/posterior, likelihood, and identity-loss integration incompletely specified. Resolve those only if baseline evidence justifies that model. Source: [[02_Projects/AIVC/project-outline]].
