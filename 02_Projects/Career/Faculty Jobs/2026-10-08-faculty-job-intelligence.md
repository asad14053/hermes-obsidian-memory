# Faculty job intelligence — 2026-10-08

## Scope, evidence and limitations

Current web searching covered Tier 1 bioengineering, biomedical informatics/data science, computational/systems biology and AI-for-science; Tier 2 computational genetics and neuroscience; and Tier 3 CS/bioinformatics and AI-for-life-sciences. University recruiting portals, medical-school and institute pages, interdisciplinary searches, and Nature, Chronicle, AcademicJobsOnline, AMIA, Science Careers and other academic boards were checked. Empty or irrelevant searches were broadened. This is a targeted sweep, not an exhaustive census.

Deduplication checked all existing Faculty Jobs reports (September 13, 21, 24, 28, October 1 and 5), the active-opportunities index, and vault-wide searches for the new institutions/search names and identifiers. No stored matching position was found for the entries below. “New” means newly discovered for this tracker, not necessarily newly advertised. Yale BIDS is an older search that remains on Yale's current open-position list.

Only NEW **STRONG POSSIBILITY** positions are saved here. No search clears the stricter APPLY threshold on the available dossier evidence. Scores, methodological bars, concerns and tailored identities are agent judgments, not hiring probabilities or verified accomplishments. Open status means the retrieved official recruitment pages invite applications; it does not establish that shortlisting has not begun. No applications or messages were submitted.
