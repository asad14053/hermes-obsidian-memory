# Faculty job intelligence — 2026-10-29

## Scope and evidence boundaries

Current searches covered Tier 1 computational biology/genomics, biomedical informatics/data science, bioengineering and AI-for-science; Tier 2 Human Genetics, medical-school genomics, neurogenomics, computational neuroscience and neurodegeneration; and targeted Tier 3 AI-for-biology/health searches. Official university career pages, medical-school and institute recruitment pages, AcademicJobsOnline, Science Careers, Nature Careers and other academic boards were checked. Promising board leads were traced to official postings where possible. This was a targeted sweep, not an exhaustive census.

All existing notes in `02_Projects/Career/Faculty Jobs` were searched by institution, title, requisition and application ID. The positions below were not previously stored. “New” means newly discovered for this tracker; the Michigan position was posted earlier in the cycle, while the WashU posting is dated October 27. No application, registration or external inquiry was submitted.

Only new **STRONG POSSIBILITY** positions are stored. No opportunity clears APPLY without a CV/publication, independence, teaching and readiness audit. An accessible official posting with a future review date supports apparent open status; it does not prove that screening has not started or that a submitted application will be competitive.

### Fit assessment — agent judgment

- **DEPARTMENT TIER:** 2 — Human Genetics / Genetics and Genomics, with explicit computational-biology strength.
- **NATURAL HOME:** HIGH.
- **FIT SCORE:** 8/10.
- **CLASSIFICATION:** STRONG POSSIBILITY.
- **WHY I FIT / DEPARTMENTAL-HOME FIT:** A broad Genetics department with stated strengths in human genetics, computational biology, epigenetics, gene regulation and technology development is a natural home for computational human-brain genomics. The McDonnell Genome Institute, Neurogenomics and Informatics Center and Center for AI-Driven Precision Medicine provide credible bridges to the proposed identity. [Search scope and environment](https://facultyopportunities.wustl.edu/Posting/Detail/1011262)
- **METHODOLOGICAL FIT:** Missing-measurement integration, donor-aware inference and preservation of disease effects are defensible computational problems. The dossier must isolate an attributable, reusable computational/statistical contribution rather than presenting use of existing imputers as method development.
- **SCIENTIFIC-DOMAIN FIT:** Human genomics, neurogenomics and disease biology are genuine institutional homes. Current SCZ/DLPFC spatial work supports the disease and tissue context, but the broad ad does not specifically prioritize psychiatric disease or spatial transcriptomics. [Official scope](https://facultyopportunities.wustl.edu/Posting/Detail/1011262)
- **MY STRONGEST MATCH:** Computational analysis of disease-associated molecular programs across human cells, donors and spatial tissue contexts.
- **MY BIGGEST GAP:** A verified independent genetics discovery or candidate-owned computational method connecting genetic variation to cellular and tissue phenotypes.
- **METHODS BAR:** Demonstrate a generalizable method or inference framework for incomplete multimodal genomic data, with protected donor-level evaluation, uncertainty/calibration and a clear genetics question. If proposing a genetics-to-cell-state program, label it as future work unless the publication record already establishes it.
- **EVIDENCE STATUS:** PARTIALLY SUPPORTED — computational/spatial genomics and SCZ are current; variant-to-function genetics, methods originality and independent-program readiness require audit.
- **SEARCH-COMMITTEE CONCERN:** The committee may see a spatial-transcriptomics analyst whose current work does not yet establish an independent genetics program or broadly reusable method.
- **TAILORED FACULTY IDENTITY:** I develop computational models connecting genetic variation, cellular state and spatial tissue organization to disease-associated molecular programs in the human brain.
- **IDENTITY CAUTION:** The genetic-variation connection is a proposed program element unless supported by publications outside the reviewed vault.
- **BEST RESEARCH STORY TO LEAD WITH:** GeneBridge/SCZ, organized around a completed computational and biological contribution; AI-genomics as the general program; AIVC only as a bounded future direction.

### Eligibility and application preparation

The posting requires a PhD or equivalent in a relevant discipline. ECE is plausible under this broad wording but is not expressly named. No minimum postdoctoral duration, work-authorization language or numerical teaching load is stated in the recovered posting. [Qualifications](https://facultyopportunities.wustl.edu/Posting/Detail/1011262)

Required materials are a one-page cover letter, CV, three-page combined research/teaching statement (one page past research, one page future plans and one page teaching), one-page commitment-to-diversity statement, and three referee contacts. References are contacted automatically only if the applicant authorizes it in the system. [Application instructions](https://facultyopportunities.wustl.edu/Posting/Detail/1011262)
