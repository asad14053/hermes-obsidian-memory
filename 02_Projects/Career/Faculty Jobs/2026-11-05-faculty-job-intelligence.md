# Faculty job intelligence — 2026-11-05

## Scope and evidence boundaries

Current web searching covered Tier 1 computational biology/genomics, quantitative biology, biomedical informatics/data science, bioengineering and AI-for-biology; Tier 2 Human Genetics, medical-school biomedical AI, neurogenomics and related biomedical departments; and selective Tier 3 computing searches with explicit computational-biology or AI-for-health tracks. Official university, medical-school and institute pages; Interfolio and other employer application pages; AcademicJobsOnline; ISCB; Science Careers; Chronicle; and related academic boards were checked. Promising board leads were traced to an employer or official institutional page where possible. This was a targeted sweep, not an exhaustive census.

All existing notes under `02_Projects/Career/Faculty Jobs` and the active-opportunities index were searched by institution, department, title and identifier. The three positions below were not previously stored. “New” means newly discovered for this tracker, not necessarily newly posted. No application, registration or external inquiry was submitted.

Only new **STRONG POSSIBILITY** positions are stored. No opportunity clears APPLY without a publication, independence, teaching and readiness audit. An accessible employer posting with an active application route or future review date supports apparent open status; it does not prove that screening has not started or that a submitted application will be competitive.

### Fit assessment — agent judgment

- **DEPARTMENT TIER:** 1 — Quantitative and Computational Biology.
- **NATURAL HOME:** HIGH.
- **FIT SCORE:** 8/10.
- **CLASSIFICATION:** STRONG POSSIBILITY.
- **WHY I FIT / DEPARTMENTAL-HOME FIT:** This is a dedicated quantitative/computational-biology department, not a general CS or wet-biology search. Current computational genomics, spatial transcriptomics and missing-measurement integration are legible as a coherent faculty program in this unit.
- **METHODOLOGICAL FIT:** The search specifically emphasizes AI method development and predictive modeling in biology. GeneBridge supplies a grounded missing-data and biological-validity problem, but the dossier must isolate a novel, reusable computational contribution rather than describe model application or benchmarking.
- **SCIENTIFIC-DOMAIN FIT:** Human genomics, cellular state, tissue organization and disease biology fit the broad biological remit. The ad does not specifically prioritize neurobiology or psychiatric disease; SCZ should be the demonstrated application, not the entire faculty identity.
- **MY STRONGEST MATCH:** Models connecting cellular state and spatial tissue organization through reliable integration of incomplete human genomic measurements.
- **MY BIGGEST GAP:** A verified candidate-owned AI or predictive-modeling method with publication-level evidence and clear independence.
- **METHODS BAR:** Demonstrate an innovative predictive-modeling or learning method for complex biological data—such as uncertainty-aware multimodal integration, structured representation learning or transportable missing-data inference—with protected donor-level evaluation, strong simple baselines and biological utility beyond the SCZ cohort.
- **EVIDENCE STATUS:** PARTIALLY SUPPORTED — the biological problem and computational-genomics work are current; original AI-method scholarship and independent-program readiness require audit.
- **SEARCH-COMMITTEE CONCERN:** The committee may see an applied spatial-genomics analyst whose strongest work benchmarks existing methods rather than an AI-method developer ready to lead an independent QCB program.
- **TAILORED FACULTY IDENTITY:** I develop computational models connecting genetic and molecular variation, cellular state and spatial tissue organization in human disease.
- **BEST RESEARCH STORY TO LEAD WITH:** GeneBridge/SCZ, framed around a verified missing-measurement and disease-effect-preservation contribution; AI-genomics as the broader program; AIVC as future work.
