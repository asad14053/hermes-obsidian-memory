# Faculty job intelligence — 2026-09-21

## Scope and interpretation

The opportunities below were checked against [[2026-09-13-faculty-job-intelligence]] and vault-wide institution/title/posting-ID searches; no matching saved position was found. “New” means newly discovered for this tracker, not necessarily newly advertised.

Fit scores and classifications are agent assessments of research alignment, not hiring probabilities or certification that every qualification is met.
