# Faculty job intelligence — 2026-10-19

## Scope, evidence and limitations

Current web searches covered Tier 1 computational biology/genomics, bioengineering, biomedical informatics/data science, systems biology and AI-for-science; Tier 2 computational human genetics and neurobiology; and Tier 3 targeted CS/EECS–biology partnerships. University and medical-school recruitment pages, an independent biomedical institute's employer application page, ISCB, AcademicJobsOnline, Science Careers, Nature Careers and other academic boards were checked. Unproductive searches and partial extractions were retried using alternative queries and official sources. This is a targeted sweep, not an exhaustive census.

All existing Faculty Jobs notes were searched by headings and content, with the active index, recent reports and older September reports reviewed for deduplication. Shortlisted names and posting IDs were also searched across the canonical vault. No saved match was found for the opportunities below. MIT `32927` is a separate Biology/Schwarzman College shared search, not previously reported Biology `32330` or Whitehead/MIT. “New” means newly discovered for this tracker, not necessarily newly advertised.

Only NEW **STRONG POSSIBILITY** opportunities are saved. No position clears APPLY on the available evidence. Scores, natural-home judgments, methods bars and recommended identities are assessments, not hiring probabilities or claims of demonstrated accomplishments. Open status is based on retrieved university/employer advertisements inviting applications and their review/deadline instructions; authenticated submission and absence of shortlisting were not tested. No application or external message was submitted.
