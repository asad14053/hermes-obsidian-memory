# Faculty job intelligence — 2026-10-15

## Scope, evidence and limitations

Current web searching covered Tier 1 computational biology, biomedical engineering, biomedical informatics/data science, systems biology and AI-for-science; Tier 2 computational human genetics and molecular/neurobiology; and Tier 3 targeted CS/bioinformatics and AI-for-biology searches. Official university and medical-school recruitment pages, an independent biomedical institute's recruitment page, ISCB and academic job boards were checked. Empty and unproductive queries were retried with broader wording. This is a targeted search, not an exhaustive census.

All existing Faculty Jobs reports were searched by headings and content; the active-opportunities index and latest report were read. Candidate institutions, titles and requisition identifiers were then searched across the canonical vault. No stored match was found for the searches below. Berkeley Computational Biomedicine `JPF05568` is distinct from previously reported EECS/AI for Biology `JPF05560`; BU BME `32490` is distinct from previously reported CDS `32632`. These are newly discovered opportunities, not necessarily newly posted.

Only new **STRONG POSSIBILITY** positions are saved here. No search clears APPLY on the available evidence. Fit scores, methods bars, concerns and proposed faculty identities are agent judgments, not hiring probabilities. Open status means current official/employer postings invite applications and show a future review/deadline; authenticated submission was not tested. BU explicitly remains pending Provost approval. No application or external message was submitted.
