# Faculty job intelligence — 2026-10-26

## Scope and evidence boundaries

Current web searches covered Tier 1 computational biology, bioengineering, biomedical informatics/data science and biostatistics; Tier 2 computational human genetics and medical-school research; and Tier 3 targeted CS/AI-for-health and neuroengineering cluster searches. Official university recruitment pages, medical-school pages, institute faculty recruitment, AcademicJobsOnline, Chronicle, Inside Higher Ed and CRA advertisements were checked. Empty or irrelevant searches were broadened; incomplete extractions were retried. This is a targeted sweep, not an exhaustive census.

All existing Faculty Jobs notes were searched for deduplication. The active index, latest report, and early reports whose entries were not fully represented in that index were read. Exact shortlisted requisitions, application routes and institution names were also searched across the canonical vault. The entries below are newly discovered, not necessarily newly posted. No applications or inquiries were submitted.

Only new STRONG POSSIBILITY positions are stored below. Scores, natural-home judgments, methods bars and committee concerns are assessments, not employer ratings or probabilities of hiring. No position clears APPLY without a publication, independence, teaching and eligibility audit. An accessible official posting soliciting applications supports apparent open status; it does not establish that shortlisting has not begun or that an authenticated submission will succeed.

Agentic experiment planning is an advertised preference, not a result the candidate can claim. AIVC architecture diagrams alone do not clear this bar.
- **EVIDENCE STATUS:** PARTIALLY SUPPORTED — current computational-genomics work supports the problem/domain; method novelty, formal training evidence and independent-program readiness require audit.
- **SEARCH-COMMITTEE CONCERN:** The committee could see an imputation/evaluation practitioner rather than a methods innovator with a publication-backed biological-engineering program.
- **TAILORED FACULTY IDENTITY:** My proposed independent program develops multimodal AI models of human cells and tissues for neuropsychiatric disease, grounded in current computational-genomics integration and validation work.
- **BEST RESEARCH STORY TO LEAD WITH:** AI-genomics anchored in completed GeneBridge/SCZ contributions; AIVC as a bounded future program, not an accomplished virtual-cell or autonomous-discovery platform.
