# Faculty job intelligence — 2026-10-12

## Scope, evidence and limitations

Current web searching covered Tier 1 biomedical engineering, computational/systems biology, biomedical informatics/data science and AI-for-science; Tier 2 computational human genetics and neurogenomics/neuroscience; and Tier 3 computing/life-sciences cluster searches. University career sites, medical-school recruitment, an independent research institute, and ISCB, Nature, Chronicle and other academic boards were checked. Failed and unproductive searches were retried with alternative queries and sources. This is a targeted sweep, not an exhaustive census.

Deduplication searched all existing Faculty Jobs notes and the active-opportunities index, then searched the canonical vault for shortlisted titles, institutions and identifiers. No stored match was found for the entries below. These are **newly discovered**, not necessarily newly posted. Yale Neuroscience is distinct from the previously reported Yale BIDS search.

Only NEW **STRONG POSSIBILITY** positions are recorded below. No position clears the stricter APPLY threshold on the available research, publication and teaching evidence. Fit scores, methods bars, concerns and proposed faculty identities are agent judgments, not hiring probabilities or claims of completed results. Open status means the identified official pages currently invite applications; authenticated submission and absence of shortlisting were not tested. No application or external message was submitted.
